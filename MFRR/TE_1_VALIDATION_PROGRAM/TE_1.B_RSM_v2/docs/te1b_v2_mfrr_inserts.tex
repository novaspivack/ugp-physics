\subsection{Reflexive Fluctuation Theorem (Existence Demonstration)}
In this section we validate the \emph{Reflexive Fluctuation Theorem} predicted by the Mathematical Foundations of Reflexive Reality. The theorem asserts that any reflexively-stabilized dynamical system—one in which local choice–point dynamics self-adjust to maintain a steady informational state—must satisfy the standard nonequilibrium fluctuation identities when expressed in terms of the reflexive entropy production $\Delta S_{\mathrm{ref}}$. At a self-consistent reflexive steady state the Jarzynski equality, the Crooks relation, and an appropriate Green--Kubo response identity must all hold concurrently for the same definition of $\Delta S_{\mathrm{ref}}$, without external fine-tuning or post hoc normalization.

To isolate and test this principle independently of substrate-specific details we constructed a minimal reflexive Markov testbed implementing the same reflexive entropy structure used throughout MFRR. The purpose of this testbed is to provide a clean existence proof; full PR-0 dynamics are \emph{not required} for the theorem itself, only a reflexive dynamical system with internal choice–point regulation and well-defined $\Delta S_{\mathrm{ref}}$.

\end{itemize}
