Why the LHC Has Found Nothing Beyond the Higgs

Since the Higgs boson was confirmed in 2012, the Large Hadron Collider has found no evidence of physics beyond the Standard Model — no supersymmetric particles, no extra dimensions, no new forces. Many BSM theories predicted particles that should already have appeared at TeV energies. They have not.

This is a confirmed GTE prediction. GTE derives the complete particle spectrum from the Z₇ winding sectors of ΦMDL: exactly five SM winding sectors (w ∈ {0,2,3,4,6}) plus the dark-mirror branch (w ∈ {1,5}). No additional sectors are available. Supersymmetric partners would require a doubling of the winding spectrum — for which the certificate structure has no mechanism. Every null LHC result is consistent with this derivation.
