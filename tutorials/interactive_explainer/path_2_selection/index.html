Selection: Why This Polynomial?

MDL doesn't just pick any rule — it eliminates 10290 candidates and arrives at one polynomial in 19 bits. This path explains every step of that elimination, why GF(7) is the only field that works, and how to read level labels on any result in the theory.

What you'll understand after this path

The selection

How MDL's five-step elimination funnel uniquely forces p = C+R−CR−LCR from an astronomically large candidate space.

Why GF(7)

Why 7 is the smallest prime where chirality (ℤ₂) and color (ℤ₃) are both algebraically derivable at zero extra cost.

The four levels

How to place any result on the Level 0–3 ladder, and why conflating a discrete algebraic fact (Level 2) with a continuum claim (Level 3) is an error.
