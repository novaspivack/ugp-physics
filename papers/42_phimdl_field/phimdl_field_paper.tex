\begin{abstract}
We study the $\ZS$-symmetric Klein--Gordon field $\PHIMDL$ as the continuum
counterpart of the Three-Layer Chiral Minkowski CA (CMCA, Paper P41).
Four principal results follow.
(1)~\textit{Poincar\'e invariance:} $\PHIMDL$ satisfies exact Lorentz
invariance $\omega^2 = k^2 + m^2$, machine-certified via
\lean{poincare\_invariance\_of\_kg} in \lean{LorentzInvariance.lean}
($\CatAL$, zero sorry).
(2)~\textit{Born rule from field amplitudes:} the position-space probability
density $P(x) = |\partial_x\Phi|^2 / \int|\partial_x\Phi|^2$ normalizes
exactly ($\CatAD$); extended to overlapping multi-kink configurations via the
canonical dual-frame inner product (\lean{born\_rule\_from\_dual\_frame\_master}
in \lean{DualFrameBornRule.lean}, $\CatAL$, zero sorry).
(3)~\textit{Physical quantum state:} the pre-measurement $\PHIMDL$ state is
the Hamiltonian thermal ensemble $P(k) = e^{-M_k/T}/Z_T$ restricted to
PSC-admissible $\ZS$ kink sectors $\{0,2,3,4,6\}$
(\lean{phimdl\_thermal\_state\_master} in \lean{PhiMDLThermalState.lean},
$\CatAL$ conditional, zero custom physics axioms); the physical state is
vacuum-dominant at observed cosmic temperatures and approaches the uniform
PSC-admissible distribution at early-universe temperatures.
(4)~\textit{3+1D extension:} $\PHIMDL$ kinks become domain walls with
tension $\sigma = 290.10\,\mathrm{MeV\,GeV}^{-2} = 7450\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}^{-2}$;
$\ZS$ superselection is preserved; the Born rule $P(k)=|c_k|^2$ is
unchanged; and SR time dilation is exact.  When two domain walls
intersect in $3+1$ dimensions, they form a junction line with exact
dimensionless tension $\lambda = -16/49$, corresponding to
$-1654.77\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}$ (attractive; ratio
$|\lambda/\sigma| = 2/m_\varphi$, pure geometry); certified in
\lean{WindingCoinDecoupling.lean} ($\CatAL$, zero sorry).
The continuum limit CMCA$\to\PHIMDL$ is machine-certified in
\lean{CMCAContinuumLimit.lean}: algebraic content is $M$-independent, the
Nyquist residual $\epsz(M) = \pi^2/(3M^2)\to 0$ as $M\to\infty$, and
$\PHIMDL$ is the unique terminal Level-1 Generative Triple Evolution (GTE) object.
Two further Lean-certified results complete the picture:
\lean{no\_finite\_ca\_exact\_lorentz\_replica} proves that every finite-$M$
discrete model has strictly positive Lorentz error ($\CatAL$, zero sorry),
and \lean{commutes\_with\_winding\_iff\_diagonal} establishes that any
$\ZS$-winding-conserving coin must be diagonal, structurally precluding
quantum interference in the discrete setting ($\CatAL$, zero sorry).
The explicit CMCA$\to\PHIMDL$ descent map is numerically confirmed at
$M=7$: Cook A-glider winding profile vs.\ BPS kink RMSD $= 5.34\%
\leq \epsz(7) = 6.71\%$, Pearson $r=0.994$.
At one loop, the kink fluctuation spectrum is the $s=1$ reflectionless
Pöschl--Teller potential ($\CatAL$) and the quantum kink mass is
$M^Q = 281 \pm 21$~MeV (interface dimensional regularization,
benchmark-validated, two independent routes; consistent with the classical
BPS mass within the scheme band).  The gauge coupling
$e^2(\Lambda_{\rm GTE}) = 7/2$ is consistent with PDG running at
$+1.5\sigma$ (tree) / $+1.3\sigma$ (quantum kink mass) once the
non-perturbatively measured kink dissolution constant
($b = 1.189 \pm 0.049$) is included, and the kink sector contributes
$\Delta\alpha_{\rm kink} = (3.5\text{--}3.7)\times10^{-4}$ to the hadronic
vacuum polarization --- a parameter-free falsifiable prediction.
\end{abstract}
