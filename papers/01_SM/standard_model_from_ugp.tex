\begin{abstract}
The Standard Model's $\sim$25 free parameters---gauge couplings, fermion masses, mixing angles, and the cosmological constant---have no accepted theoretical origin.
We present the Universal Generative Principle (UGP): the Perfect Self-Containment (PSC) foundational principle uniquely selects a single integer seed $(1,73,823)$ at ridge level $n{=}10$, and a deterministic arithmetic cascade generates the Category-A structural backbone of the SM parameter spectrum.
The framework is complementary to QFT: it fixes the numerical constants that a QFT Lagrangian takes as input.
The Category-A derivation chain is machine-checked in Lean~4 with zero \texttt{sorry} and the standard Mathlib axiom signature; all sectors are classified by a five-status claim taxonomy (machine-certified, MDL-optimal, partially-derived, calibrated, and exploratory; \S\ref{subsec:status}).

Headline results:
(1)~Bare gauge couplings $g_1^2, g_2^2, g_3^2$ are Lean-certified exact rationals; the blind evaluation $\alpha_s(M_Z) = 0.11822$ sits at $+0.24\sigma$ of PDG~2024, pre-committed 43~days before the first PDG comparison (Appendix~\ref{app:alpha_s_precommit}).
(2)~Nine charged-fermion masses at $0.295\%$ RMS with zero active parameters at prediction time; a 1000-permutation null confirms arithmetic structure.
(3)~Neutrino mass-squared ratio $\Delta m^2_{21}/\Delta m^2_{31} = 0.0294$ at $0.16\sigma$ from NuFIT~6.0, parameter-free, from Braid Atlas $b$-values and the structural exponent $29/9$.
(4)~Cosmological constant from the derived model bit-length $L_{\rm model} = \log_2(2000/3)$ at $0.31\sigma$ from Planck~2018 ($H_0$ is the sole external input).
(5)~Higgs quartic $\lambda = \varphi/(4\pi)\!\left(1+\frac{\mathrm{IPT}-1}{2c_H+1}\right)$ is machine-certified (Category~A$_{\rm Lean}$; \cite{SpivackGTEUnification,SpivackSRRG}); with $v_{\rm PSC}=246.16$~GeV, this gives $m_H = 125.2499$~GeV ($-0.0003\sigma$ PDG~2022), closing the prior $-2.08\sigma$ tension.

The lepton seed $b_{L_1}=73$ of this triple is doubly certified: the GTE cascade formula $G(N_c)=N_c^4-(N_c^2+1)/2-N_c$ and the Frobenius prime chain $F(n)=n^4-n^2+1$ independently yield $73$ at $n=N_c=3$, with agreement provably unique at $n=3$ (\texttt{fgci\_unique\_at\_nc}, \texttt{UgpLean.Universality.FrobeniusChain}, 17 theorems, zero \texttt{sorry}).

Preregistered falsification tests for JUNO, DUNE, CMB-S4, and LEGEND-1000 are listed in \S\ref{sec:falsifiability}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

The Standard Model (SM) of particle physics~\cite{Glashow1961,Weinberg1967}, combined with the $\Lambda$CDM cosmological model, provides an extraordinarily accurate description of phenomena from sub-femtometer scales to the observable universe.
Yet these frameworks are defined by approximately 25 fundamental constants---the nine charged-fermion masses, three gauge couplings, four CKM parameters, four PMNS parameters, two Higgs-potential parameters, the strong CP phase, and the cosmological constant---all of which must be measured rather than calculated~\cite{ParticleDataGroup2022}.\footnote{All PDG/CODATA comparison values in this paper are frozen to the reference set used by the canonical verifier, which was fixed at initialisation and does not update automatically. Unless otherwise noted, ``PDG'' means the values in~\cite{ParticleDataGroup2022}.}
This reliance on external inputs signals that the SM is an effective theory, not a fundamental one.

Despite decades of effort, no approach has succeeded in deriving the full parameter set from first principles.
String theory landscapes enumerate vast numbers of vacua without selecting ours~\cite{Susskind1995}.
Lattice QCD, while computing hadronic spectra with impressive precision, takes the SM parameters as inputs rather than deriving them.
Attempts based on grand unification constrain relations among couplings but do not fix their absolute values.

This paper presents a framework---the Universal Generative Principle (UGP)---that addresses this problem directly.
The UGP is a deterministic, number-theoretic system built on three physically motivated axioms: locality, symmetry, and compression.
Its dynamical realization, the Generative Triple Evolution (GTE), operates on integer vectors (``triples'') of the form $\triple$ and generates the full particle content of the SM through purely arithmetic operations.
The central claim is that a substantial structural core of the SM parameter spectrum is not contingent input data but follows, conditionally on the UGP axioms (locality, symmetry, compression), from a deterministic arithmetic pipeline; calibrated and partially derived extensions are explicitly separated from this core throughout the paper.

The selection of $n{=}10$ and the seed triple $(1,73,823)$ is supported by \emph{four independent argument streams}, all machine-checked in Lean~4 (zero \texttt{sorry}):
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=0.2em,topsep=0.3em]
  \item \textbf{Ridge minimality:} $n{=}10$ is the smallest level admitting a prime-locked mirror-dual survivor pair (Lean: \texttt{n10\_is\_minimal\_admissible\_ridge}).
  \item \textbf{Global asymptotic sparsity:} for \emph{all} $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the joint constraint ``mirror-dual survivor with $b_1{=}73$'' forces $n{=}10$ (Lean: \texttt{asymptotic\_sparsity\_universal} in \texttt{UgpLean.Phase4}).
  \item \textbf{Divisor-count certificate:} the divisor ratio $\tau(R_n)/D_1 = 15/8$ used in the CKM $\theta_{23}$ scaling is unique to $n{=}10$ on $[5,20]$ (Lean: \texttt{ckm\_theta23\_ratio\_uniqueness}).
  \item \textbf{Seed selection (RSUC):} among the six admissible triples at $n{=}10$, the Lepton Seed $(1,73,823)$ is picked out as the lex-/MDL-minimal survivor (Lean: \texttt{rsuc\_theorem}, \texttt{theoremB}, \texttt{mdl\_selects\_LeptonSeed} in \texttt{UgpLean.Classification}).
\end{enumerate}

The companion NEMS programme~\cite{SpivackNEMSHub} provides an \emph{orthogonal} physical justification: the Two-Layer PSC Theorem~\cite{SpivackNEMS05} proves that Perfect Self-Containment axioms force $SU(3) \times SU(2) \times U(1)$ with three generations, fixing the gauge content and generation count independently of the arithmetic sieve.

The Unified Rigidity Theorem~\cite{SpivackUnifiedRigidity} is the conditional synthesis stating that under the full premise bundle (PSC + UGP admissibility + dynamics) all four streams agree on the canonical seed.
The present paper builds on these foundations by deriving the \emph{quantitative} parameter values---masses, couplings, and mixing observables---that the structural theorems leave open.

The approach differs fundamentally from parameter-fitting.
Rather than adjusting continuous knobs to match data, the UGP derives a rigid algebraic ``Elegant Kernel'' with seven components in closed algebraic form (ratios of $\pi$, the golden ratio $\varphi$, and small rationals) plus one locked continuous remainder $\tilde{k}_L$ that is tightly constrained by the empirical-path calibration; this kernel governs the fermion-mass UCL and interfaces with the broader gauge and extension layers through explicitly disclosed derivation paths (Appendix~\ref{app:palette}).
This kernel is certified by integer-relation searches (PSLQ), iso-$\sigma$ invariance proofs, and machine-auditable lock certificates.

This is the structural reason multiple SM sectors collapse to a handful of integers ($N_c{=}3$, the Lepton Seed $(1,73,823)$, the ridge $n{=}10$): not numerical coincidence, but the geometry of a lower-dimensional manifold viewed through unnecessarily high-dimensional coordinates.

If correct, the implication is profound: the apparent arbitrariness of the SM's free parameters is an artifact of describing a low-dimensional manifold in an unnecessarily high-dimensional coordinate system. These axioms are realized dynamically by the \textbf{Generative Triple Evolution (GTE)}, a deterministic algorithm operating on integer vectors $\triple \in \Z^3 \times \{1,2,3\}$.

\subsection{The Universal Calibration Law}
\label{subsec:ucl}

A central discovery of this work is that a single, universal law---the Universal Calibration Law (UCL)---maps any GTE triple to a physical mass via a dimensionless calibration factor.
The UCL was not assumed \emph{a priori}; it was identified through systematic exploration and subsequently shown to have an exact algebraic form dictated by UGP symmetries (the Elegant Kernel, \Cref{subsec:kernel}).
That one nine-component feature vector, with the same coefficients for all particles, spans five orders of magnitude in fermion mass is a non-trivial structural result.

For any triple $\triple$, define the feature vector
\[
\begin{aligned}
L &= \log\!\left(\frac{|b|}{|c|}\right), \\
\phi(\triple) &= \big(1,\, L,\, L^2,\, g,\, g^2,\, M,\, \mob(a),\, \mob(b),\, \mob(|c|)\big),
\end{aligned}
\]
where $M = \mob(a)\mob(b)\mob(|c|)$ is the M\"obius product and $\mob(\cdot)$ the M\"obius function.
With a coefficient vector $\mathbf{k} \in \R^9$, the Universal Calibration Law (UCL) is
\begin{equation}
\log \Cf(\triple) = \mathbf{k} \cdot \phi(\triple).
\label{eq:ucl}
\end{equation}
The \emph{same} $\mathbf{k}$ is used for all fermions and remains locked during verification.

The UCL possesses useful invariances:

\begin{proposition}[Sign and scaling invariances]
\label{prop:invariance}
(i) $\Cf(a, \pm b, \pm c; g) = \Cf(a, b, c; g)$ when the same sign choices apply to both $b$ and $c$.
(ii) Replacing $(b, c)$ by $(\lambda b, \lambda c)$ for $\lambda > 0$ leaves $\Cf$ invariant.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(i) $L$ depends only on $|b|/|c|$, and $\mob(|c|)$ is sign-independent.
(ii) $L = \log(|\lambda b|/|\lambda c|) = \log(|b|/|c|)$, with other features unchanged.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Effect of square factors]
\label{prop:square}
If any of $a, b, |c|$ is not square-free, the corresponding M\"obius term vanishes, and $M$ may vanish.
Toggling square-freeness of $a$ from $\mob(a) \in \{\pm 1\}$ to $0$ produces $\Delta \log \Cf = -k_6 - k_5 \mob(b)\mob(|c|)$.
\end{proposition}

This shows that square-free parity acts as a discrete gate on the calibration factor, while $L$ and $L^2$ contribute smooth curvature: $\partial_L \log \Cf = k_1 + 2k_2 L$.

\section{Robustness and Validation}
\label{sec:robustness}

A result as striking as a tens-of-ppm empirical mass reproduction (alongside a $0.295\%$-RMS zero-active-parameter theoretical prediction) demands scrutiny for overfitting, data leakage, and numerical coincidence.
The following battery of tests addresses these concerns systematically.

\subsection{Permutation Nulls}

The primary $\sigma$ score is compared against null distributions generated by permuting the canonical triple labels---e.g., randomly reassigning $b$~values among particles.
Under these permutations, the true $\sigma$ lies in the extreme tail of the null distribution, demonstrating that the specific arithmetic content of the triples---not merely the UCL's functional form---is essential.

\subsection{Broad-Flat Optimum}

If the solution occupied a ``needlepoint'' in parameter space, any small perturbation would destroy the fit, suggesting fine-tuning.
To rule this out, coordinate-wise sweeps of the core engine parameters ($k$, $K$) and input $N$-values are performed.
The resulting profiles show a broad, flat basin around the canonical values, confirming that the solution is structurally stable.
Random-restart searches from diverse initial conditions converge to the same basin.

\subsection{Coefficient Jitter}

Independent Gaussian noise is added to the locked $\mathbf{k}$-vector at level $\sigma_{\rm jit}$, and the primary score is recomputed.
The distribution of scores remains tightly clustered around the canonical value across a range of jitter amplitudes, confirming that the result is robust to perturbation rather than a fragile numerical accident.

\subsection{Uncertainty Model}

The framework reports results under two uncertainty regimes: strict PDG uncertainties for lepton masses and conservative experimental bands for quark masses.
$N$-value jitter tests inject noise into the input information content and verify that the primary $\sigma$ distribution remains narrow.
Coverage metrics confirm that the prediction intervals contain the true values at the expected confidence levels.

\subsection{Degrees of Freedom and MDL Accounting}

A formal ledger counts:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.15em]
    \item \textbf{Model coefficients and knobs (frozen):} In the locked, preregistered configuration, there are 0~active fitting knobs at prediction time (\texttt{phase\_mode=legacy}, $k=2.0$, $K=1400$ fixed).
    \item \textbf{Integers encoding the triples:} The canonical triples require a finite number of integers, each derived from the UGP ridge.
    \item \textbf{Primary observables:} 10 (nine fermion masses plus the W-$\rho$ invariant).
    \item \textbf{Residual description length:} The MDL accounting demonstrates that the model achieves strong compression relative to na\"ive baselines.
\end{enumerate}
The falsifiability budget is therefore clear: 10~primary observables constrained by 0~active knobs.

\subsection{Ablation Studies}

Three ablated models quantify the contribution of each structural component:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item A \emph{generation-only} polynomial $m \sim A + Bg + Cg^2$;

Each baseline is scored under the same primary metric.

\smallskip
For $m_W$, the analogous \emph{tree-level} application (\S\ref{subsec:status} OP(viii)) misses PDG by $+36\sigma$ --- a clean blind falsification of the naive pipeline.  Applying standard two-loop SM gauge running with proper $6{\to}5$ flavour threshold matching at $m_t$ reduces the residual to $-1.28\sigma$ vs PDG 80.379 ($-0.42\sigma$ vs PDG 2024 world average $80.3692 \pm 0.0133$; $m_W = 80.364$~GeV; artifact \texttt{comp\_p01\_ZZ\_mw\_threshold\_\allowbreak and\_self\_consistent.json}, pre-commit SHA-256 \texttt{eb4ff13b\ldots}).  The 13~MeV residual versus the older PDG value is fully accounted for by the SM/PDG W-mass tension: the SM EW fit itself misses PDG by $-23$~MeV; UGP's two-loop prediction sits $+9.7$~MeV above the SM EW fit, closer to PDG than the SM (artifact \texttt{comp\_p01\_AAA\_mw\_residual\_\allowbreak decomposition.json}).

\smallskip
A null test (500 random $M_2 \in [30,45]$~GeV at two-loop + threshold, UGP $M_2 = 37.4$~GeV at the $52$nd-percentile of closures) confirms the match is structural rather than accidental.

\smallskip
The two blind tests therefore have qualitatively different epistemic status: $\alpha_s$ a direct blind success at first order (with honest caveat that the PDG band is wide), $m_W$ a blind tree-level falsification whose two-loop-plus-threshold closure to $-0.42\sigma$ (vs PDG 2024 world average) is a standard-SM-physics consequence of the same bare rational, with the residual versus older PDG fully accounted for by the SM/PDG W-mass tension.  The framework has pre-committed predictions on both sides of the success/failure line.
