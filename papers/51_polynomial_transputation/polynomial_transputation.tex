\begin{abstract}
The quantum measurement problem --- why a superposition produces a single
definite outcome, and why with probability $|c_k|^2$ --- remains unresolved
across standard interpretations of quantum mechanics, which either postulate
collapse, multiply unobservable branches, or explain apparent classicality
without resolving outcomes.
This paper provides a formal derivation of the measurement mechanism from
first principles within the GTE (Generative Triple Evolution) framework.

The GTE polynomial $p(L,C,R) = C+R-CR-LCR$ over $\GF{7}$, the unique
Minimum Description Length (MDL)-minimal encoding of the Standard Model
generation structure, carries a complete formal certificate of the quantum
measurement mechanism.
Its Perfect Self-Containment (PSC) projection is \fmdl{}, the discrete
adjudicator that defines the measurement orbit
$\mathrm{GEN}_1 \to \mathrm{GEN}_2 \to \mathrm{GEN}_3 \to \mathrm{VAC}$~---~the
only PSC-consistent trajectory in the cellular automaton dynamics~---~and
this projection is characterized in three parts: \fmdl{} agrees with $p$ on
the binary sector $\{0,1\}^3$ (Rule~110), overrides $p$ on the ten
SM-orbit neighborhoods to enforce generation-orbit consistency, and projects
to zero on all remaining 325 neighborhoods.
The polynomial's real diagonal encodes the Self-Referential Renormalization
Group (SRRG) adjudication fixed point $\varphi = (\sqrt{5}-1)/2 \approx 0.618$
(machine-certified, \CatAL).
A Three-Level MDL Unification Theorem (\CatAD) shows that
$\Ptop(X) = \arg\min_{x \in X} K(x \mid \text{PSC})$ operates at three nested,
non-circular domains: theory selection ($p$ from rule space), field dynamics
($\PHIMDL$ via the PMDL variational principle), and quantum event adjudication
(Born rule via transputation).
The polynomial $p$ is the connecting object: it \emph{is} the output of
Level~1, enters the PMDL action at Level~2, and its PSC-projection \fmdl{}
defines the orbit used at Level~3.
A two-sided computability classification (\CatAD{}; hardness core \CatAL)

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The measurement problem~---~how quantum superposition becomes a definite
outcome~---~remains unresolved in most interpretations of quantum mechanics.
Standard frameworks postulate measurement as an axiom (Copenhagen~\cite{Bohm1952}),
multiply branches without a selection mechanism (Everett~\cite{Everett1957}),
rely on decoherence without resolving outcomes~\cite{Zurek2003},
or postulate physical collapse~\cite{GhirardiRiminiWeber1986}.
CA-based interpretations of QM also exist~\cite{tHooft2016CA,Wolfram2002}.
This paper provides a formal derivation of the measurement mechanism from
first principles within the GTE framework: quantum measurement is not added
as an axiom but is forced~---~by PSC closure and diagonal self-reference~---~as
a consequence of the universe being a record-bearing, reflexive computational
system \cite{SpivackGTECompleteFramework,SpivackQMFromR110}.

The GTE polynomial $p(L,C,R) = C+R-CR-LCR$ over $\GF{7}$ is, from first
principles, the unique MDL-minimal CA rule consistent with the Standard Model
generation structure and PSC \cite{SpivackGTEPolynomialWolfram,SpivackGTEPolynomialUFT}.

P49~\cite{SpivackGTEPolynomialWolfram} proves the four-object tower
$p \to \fmdl{} \to \CMCA \to \PHIMDL$ and the invariant-subset uniqueness
of $\fmdl{}$; P37~\cite{SpivackQMFromR110} derives the full Hilbert space

($D_1$--$D_5$ constraints, forcing theorems)~---~the physical realization of the
abstract formal theory of transputation~\cite{SpivackNEMS76,SpivackNEMS77}~---~and
the MDL three-roles non-circularity argument (P48 \S5, ``The Three Faces of MDL'').

\subsection{Information-Theoretic Gap}
\label{sec:info_gap}

The PSC-projection $p \to \fmdl{}$ has two distinct information-theoretic
signatures.

\paragraph{Algorithmic complexity gap (\CatAL, \CatA{}).}
The MDL description of $p$ requires 19 bits
(\lean{K\_CA\_CMCA}, \CatAL; the family-level MDL optimality of the
$\GF{7}$ substrate is \lean{mdl\_ca\_rule\_coding\_closed}, \CatAL, P49)
\cite{SpivackGTEPolynomialWolfram}. The MDL description of \fmdl{} requires
approximately 50 bits (the 8-entry Rule~110 table plus the 10 orbit-override
entries, each with position and value). The algorithmic K-gap is
$K(\fmdl{}) - K(p) \approx 31$ bits~\cite{LiVitanyi2008,RissanenMDL}: the
PSC orbit specification is more expensive to encode than the polynomial itself.

\paragraph{Distributional KL divergence (\CatA{}).}
Treating $p$ and $\fmdl{}$ as probability distributions over $\GF{7}^3$
(normalized by the sum of positive outputs), the KL divergence is:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{KL}(\tilde{f}_{\mathrm{MDL}} \;\|\; \tilde{p}) \;\approx\; 4.6\text{--}7.7 \text{ bits},
\end{equation}
depending on the model for the SM-orbit entries (Model~A: binary only;
Model~B: binary plus orbit windows). The reverse divergence
$\mathrm{KL}(\tilde{p} \;\|\; \tilde{f}_{\mathrm{MDL}}) \approx 24$--$25$~bits
reflects the much higher entropy of $p$ as a distribution.

The KL divergence and K-gap are not proportional: KL measures
distributional discrepancy at the Shannon level~\cite{Shannon1948,CoverThomas2006}
(how different the output histograms are over $\GF{7}^3$), while the K-gap
measures the structural overhead of specifying \emph{which} inputs are
PSC-admissible. Both quantify aspects of the $p \to \fmdl{}$ gap, but at
fundamentally different levels of description.

The distributional non-equivalence is machine-certified:
\begin{theorem}[\CatAL\ — \lean{kl\_divergence\_fmdl\_p\_nonzero}]
There exists a triple $(L,C,R) \in \GF{7}^3$ such that
$p(L,C,R) \neq \fmdl{}(L,C,R)$, certifying that $p$ and $\fmdl{}$ are
distinct as distributions over $\GF{7}^3$.
\end{theorem}

\section{The SRRG Fixed-Point Bridge}
\label{sec:srrg_bridge}

The GTE polynomial over $\mathbb{R}$ (extending $p$ from $\GF{7}$ to $\mathbb{R}$
by the natural embedding) yields the real-valued function
$p_{\mathbb{R}}(L,C,R) = C+R-CR-LCR$. Its diagonal restriction is:
\begin{equation}
  p_{\mathbb{R}}(x,x,x) = 2x - x^2 - x^3.
\end{equation}
The fixed-point equation $p_{\mathbb{R}}(x,x,x) = x$ reduces to:
\begin{equation}
  x^3 + x^2 - x = 0
  \;\Longrightarrow\;
  x\bigl(x^2 + x - 1\bigr) = 0,
\end{equation}
with roots $x = 0$, $x = (\sqrt{5}-1)/2 \approx 0.618$, and
$x = -(1+\sqrt{5})/2 \approx -1.618$. The unique positive real root is
\begin{equation}
  \varphi \;=\; \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} \;\approx\; 0.618034.
\end{equation}

This value is identically the \emph{SRRG adjudication fixed point}:
the Self-Referential Renormalization Group (P27~\cite{SpivackSRRG}) identifies
the self-consistent running-coupling fixed point $g^* = (\sqrt{5}-1)/2$
(equivalently $1/\varphi_{\mathrm{large}}$ with
$\varphi_{\mathrm{large}} = (1+\sqrt{5})/2 \approx 1.618$).

\begin{theorem}[\CatAL\ — \lean{gte\_poly\_srrg\_bridge}]
\label{thm:srrg_bridge}
The unique positive real fixed point of $p_{\mathbb{R}}(x,x,x) = x$ is
$\varphi = (\sqrt{5}-1)/2$, identically equal to the SRRG adjudication
fixed point $g^*$. Machine-certified in \texttt{srrg-lean}

\subsubsection{The Two-Sided Classification}
\label{sec:two_sided}

Proposition~\ref{prop:comp_gap} is a lower bound only: it says transputation
exceeds total-effective computation, but does not say by how much, leaving
open the misreading that transputation is hypercomputation.
The following classification closes the gap from both sides.
It locates the adjudication function exactly: at the halting degree
$\mathbf{0}'$, in the Gold--Putnam ``trial-and-error''
stratum~\cite{Gold1965,Putnam1965}, and no higher.
The abstract form of this result~---~that a closed, record-bearing,
diagonal-capable system forces an internal adjudicator whose decision
content sits at degree $\mathbf{0}'$ and is realized as a Putnam--Gold
trial-and-error limit~---~is the subject of the NEMS formal theory of
transputation~\cite{SpivackNEMS76}; the theorems below are its
GTE-specific physical realization, with DSAC as the concrete $[D]$-member
(\cite{SpivackNEMS77}, \S\ref{sec:dsac}).

The classification concerns the adjudication layer $[D]$, not the substrate
levels below it: the record fragment and the finite winding-sector
adjudication are defined over \fmdl{} (Object~1, the discrete adjudicator),
and $\Ptop_D$/DSAC is the continuum $[D]$-member operating above $\PHIMDL$
(Object~3/T). The degree statements below are properties of the adjudication
function over these levels~---~not of the polynomial $p$ (Object~0) or of
the CMCA tape architecture (Object~2).

\paragraph{The premise bundle.}
The upper bound is conditional on five named structural premises. They are
explicit hypotheses about the GTE record theory~---~not hidden
assumptions~---~and each names a specific attack surface for refutation.

\begin{definition}[Premise bundle PR1--PR5]
\label{def:premise_bundle}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item[PR1 (Finite-witness record truth):] record-truth on the diagonal
    fragment is $\Sigma^0_1$: every record-inscribed fact has a finite
    witness. Physics writes only $\Sigma_1$ sentences into records~---~a
    finite stable configuration certifies an occurrence, but no finite
    record certifies a non-occurrence over unbounded time. (The
    framework's own physical instantiation: vacuum reachability under
    \fmdl{} is $\Sigma^0_1$-complete, P34~\cite{SpivackGTEMobius}.)
  \item[PR2 (Effective candidate enumeration):] a Turing machine
    enumerates the admissible continuations $S(w)$ of a record $w$.
    This is constitutive of the transputation class as defined in
    P34~\cite{SpivackGTEMobius}.
  \item[PR3 (Finite sector structure with MDL-conservativity):]
    (a)~the record-equivalence quotient of $S(w)$ is finite~---~adjudication
    outcomes are winding-sector labels drawn from the finite PSC-admissible
    list; (b)~dissonance comparisons are computable on enumerated
    candidates; (c)~among unrefuted candidates, positing an unwitnessed
    event strictly raises the description cost~---~the operational content
    of $\Ptop(X) = \arg\min K(x \mid \text{PSC})$.
  \item[PR4 ($\Pi_1$ admissibility / $\Sigma_1$ refutation):]
    candidate--record inconsistency manifests at a finite stage;
    admissibility is $\Pi^0_1$.
  \item[PR5 (Record readout):] at diagonal choice points, record-distinct
    branches are computably labeled by the coded statement's truth bit.
\end{description}
The refutation surface is exactly: physically inscribed $\Pi_2$ facts
(against PR1) or infinite adjudication sectors (against PR3(a)).
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Hardness.}
The lower bound needs no new mathematics: the machine-certified halting
bridge of the Arithmetic Self-Reference structure is already a many-one
reduction.

\begin{theorem}[$\Sigma^0_1$-completeness of diagonal record-truth;
  \CatAD{}, hardness core \CatAL]
\label{thm:rt_complete}
Under the Arithmetic Self-Reference structure and PR1, record-truth
restricted to the diagonal fragment, $\mathrm{RT}{\upharpoonright}\mathrm{diag}$,
is $\Sigma^0_1$-complete under many-one reduction: it is many-one
equivalent to the halting problem, lies in $\Delta^0_2 \setminus \Delta^0_1$,
has Turing degree exactly $\mathbf{0}'$, and is 1-c.e.\ (one mind change
suffices: guess ``false,'' switch when the witness arrives, never switch
back).
The hardness direction is machine-certified unconditionally
(\lean{halting\_manyOne\_reducible\_to\_RT}, \CatAL); the membership

\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Upper bound.}

\begin{theorem}[Limit computability of the adjudicator;
  \CatAD{} conditional on PR1--PR4]
\label{thm:limit_computable}
Under PR1--PR4, with $D \in [D]$ satisfying $D_1$--$D_5$, the sector-valued
adjudicator is limit-computable: there is a total computable stage function
$g$ with
\begin{equation}
  \Ptop_D(w) \;=\; \lim_{s \to \infty} g(w,s),
\end{equation}
and the number of mind changes (stages $s$ with $g(w,s+1) \neq g(w,s)$) is
at most $2(k_w - 1)$, where $k_w$ is the number of admissible winding
sectors of the record $w$. For single-kink adjudication events
($k_w \leq 5$), at most $8$ mind changes occur.
By the Shoenfield limit lemma~\cite{Shoenfield1959},
$\Ptop_D \leq_T \emptyset'$: transputation never exceeds the first
Turing jump.
The stage construction is machine-certified with the stage-convergence
hypothesis as a named structure
(\lean{pt\_limit\_computable}, transputation-lean; see
Appendix~\ref{app:lean} for the disclosure of its conditionality).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
By PR2 and PR4 there are computable predicates $\mathrm{alive}(w,i,s)$
(non-increasing in $s$) and $\mathrm{wit}(w,i,s)$ ($\Sigma_1$ witness
flags), obtained by running the computable dynamics $s$ steps against each
candidate's asserted record. Define $g(w,s)$ as the dissonance-argmin over
the alive candidates enumerated by stage $s$, with MDL-conservativity
(PR3(c)) charging unwitnessed posited events. Since $k_w$ is finite
(PR3(a)), there is a stage after which all sectors have enumerated and all
arriving witnesses have arrived; sector-distinguishing facts are $\Sigma_1$
(PR1), so candidates omitting an arrived witness are refuted, while
candidates positing a never-arriving witness are never $D$-minimal. The
conservative argmin is therefore eventually constant and equals the unique
$D_4$-minimum. Each mind change is a new-sector arrival or a
refutation/witness flip, giving the $2(k_w-1)$ bound.
The MDL-conservativity premise carries the bound in the asymmetric case:
refutation of the positing candidate is $\Pi_1$ and never fires at a finite
stage; it is the $\arg\min K(x \mid \text{PSC})$ structure itself that
keeps the approximation on the conservative branch and makes the limit
correct.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Degree completeness; \CatAD{} conditional on PR5]
\label{thm:degree_complete}
Under the Arithmetic Self-Reference structure and PR5,
$\emptyset' \leq_T \Ptop_D{\upharpoonright}\mathrm{diag}$. Combined with
Theorems~\ref{thm:rt_complete} and~\ref{thm:limit_computable}:
\begin{equation}
  \deg_T\!\bigl(\Ptop_D{\upharpoonright}\mathrm{diag}\bigr)
  \;=\;
  \deg_T\!\bigl(\mathrm{RT}{\upharpoonright}\mathrm{diag}\bigr)
  \;=\; \mathbf{0}' \quad \text{exactly.}
\end{equation}
The adjudication function on the diagonal fragment is
$\Delta^0_2$-Turing-complete, in the bounded-mind-change stratum: quantum
measurement is trial-and-error computation in Putnam's exact

The many-one form is machine-certified

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
PR5 labels diagonal branches by the coded statement's truth bit; $D_2$

\paragraph{Operational consequence.}

\begin{theorem}[No certified hypercomputation from measurement; \CatAL{}
  anchor, unconditional]
\label{thm:no_modulus}
No computable modulus of convergence exists for the stage sequence $g$: a
computable stage bound $s_0(n)$ would make
$\chi_{\mathrm{RT}}(n) = g(n, s_0(n))$ computable, contradicting
\lean{asr\_rt\_not\_computable} (\CatAL).
Consequently ``adjudication complete'' is not an internal observable, and
no certified $\Sigma^0_1$ decision is extractable from finitely many
physical events. Machine-certified:
\lean{no\_computable\_convergence\_modulus} (\CatAL, nems-lean).
\end{theorem}

Putnam's observation that a trial-and-error solver is ``never sure'' it has
the correct answer~\cite{Putnam1965} is here upgraded to a theorem about
physical measurement: nature converges to the right adjudication outcome
after finitely many revisions, but no physical protocol can certify, at any
finite stage, that convergence has occurred.

\begin{corollary}[One-jump universe; \CatAD{} conditional on PR1--PR5]
\label{cor:one_jump}
Physics requires exactly one Turing jump. The adjudication function is
strictly above degree $\mathbf{0}$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:comp_gap}) and
exactly at degree $\mathbf{0}'$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:degree_complete});
physics never requires $\emptyset''$. The cap is enforced by the record
theory itself: realized adjudication outcomes are ordinary finite records
($\Sigma_1$ facts about realized history, so PR1 applies to them in turn),
while $\Pi_2$ counterfactual-limit statements are never physically
inscribed~---~jump iteration through nested adjudication is blocked.
This is falsifiable in principle: any physical process of Turing degree
strictly above $\mathbf{0}'$ refutes the GTE adjudication architecture.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Bounded mind changes]
The classification places transputation in the bounded-mind-change
(Ershov) stratum: diagonal record-truth is 1-c.e., diagonal selection
needs at most one revision, and compound single-kink adjudication needs at
most $2(k_w-1) \leq 8$. Transputation is simultaneously as informative as
the halting problem (degree $\mathbf{0}'$) and as tame as a
finitely-revisable guesser~---~the precise mathematical form of ``lawful
but non-algorithmic.'' (There is no tension: $\emptyset'$ itself is
1-c.e.) The stage-convergence structure is verified computationally
(\CatA): the diagonal family converges with exactly one mind change at
the halting time; $500/500$ compound-record trials converge within the
bound (maximum observed: 3 mind changes); and convergence stages exhibit
the heavy-tailed, modulus-free phenomenology of
Theorem~\ref{thm:no_modulus} (Appendix~\ref{app:code}).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Thermodynamic cost of transputation]
\label{rem:landauer}
The mind-change bound $2(k_w - 1) \leq 8$ converts directly into a
machine-certified thermodynamic prediction via Landauer's principle
(\lean{landauer\_heat\_floor\_transputation}, \lean{landauer\_heat\_ceiling\_transputation},
\CatAL; \texttt{Measurement/LandauerFloor}, ugp-lean).
With $k_w = 5$ PSC-admissible kink sectors, each single-kink transputation
event erases $\ln 5$ bits of alternative winding at cost
$Q_{\min} = k_B T \ln 5 \approx 1.61\,k_B T$, giving a floor on the
thermodynamic heat released per adjudication event. The full
$2(k_w - 1) = 8$ mind-change budget gives the ceiling
$Q_{\max} = 8 k_B T \ln 2$. Both bounds are
falsifiable: any single-kink adjudication event dissipating less than
$k_B T \ln 5$ would contradict Landauer's principle as applied to the
GTE five-sector structure.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Structure of the computability gap]
The level-raising map $\pi_{\PSC}^{(0)} \to \pi_{\PSC}^{(\infty)}$ is a
\emph{structural correspondence}, not a categorical functor. A functor
would require morphism-preservation, but the computable morphisms at
Level~$0\to1$ map to non-computable morphisms at Level~$2\to3$; no
structure-preserving map exists across the computability boundary.
The correspondence is therefore at the level of MDL semantics:
both levels implement $\arg\min K(x \mid \text{PSC})$, and
non-computability emerges in the continuum limit.
\end{remark}

The SRRG fixed point $\varphi$ provides a \emph{numerical bridge} across
this computability gap: it is simultaneously the real-diagonal fixed point
of $p$ at Level~0 (Theorem~\ref{thm:srrg_bridge}, \CatAL) and the
self-consistent adjudication attractor of the SRRG at Level~2
(P27~\cite{SpivackSRRG}, \CatAL). The polynomial carries the adjudication
answer in its algebraic structure, even though computing that answer in the
full quantum case requires non-computable adjudication.

\begin{remark}[Significance of Three-Level Unification]
The significance of the Three-Level MDL Unification is not merely structural
elegance.
The same MDL principle that selects the theory from an astronomically large
rule space also governs how that theory evolves as a field and how it
adjudicates quantum events.
These are not three separate applications of an information-theoretic
principle~---~they are three domains in which a self-contained universe
necessarily applies the same self-description criterion.
The computability gap between Level~$0\to1$ (computable PSC-restriction) and
Level~$2\to3$ (non-computable transputation) is not an inconsistency~---~it
is the formal content of the Physical Incompleteness Theorem (analogous to
G\"{o}del's incompleteness~\cite{Godel1931} and Turing's
undecidability~\cite{Turing1936}): a universe powerful enough to describe
itself cannot compute all of its own event outcomes.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The Level-3 residual classified: the cosmological constant

\end{remark}

\section{The Measurement Problem: GTE vs.\ Prior Frameworks}
\label{sec:qm_comparison}

Quantum mechanics, as ordinarily formulated, requires an external rule~---~the
Born rule together with wavefunction collapse~---~grafted onto unitary
Schr\"odinger evolution.
Unitary dynamics alone never selects a unique outcome; something outside the
formalism must be invoked.
This is the \emph{measurement problem}.

\paragraph{Copenhagen / Bohr.}
The Copenhagen interpretation defines measurement by reference to a classical
apparatus, without accounting for the quantum--classical boundary.
In GTE, that boundary \emph{is} the Level~$0\to1$ transition ($p \to \fmdl{}$):
the ``classical apparatus'' corresponds to the PSC-admissible orbit filter.

\paragraph{Many-worlds / Everett.}
Everettian quantum mechanics~\cite{Everett1957} eliminates collapse: all branches
exist, with the Born rule supplied by decision theory or branch-counting
arguments rather than derivation.
GTE's response: transputation is the unique PSC-forced branch selector.
GTE does not branch~---~it selects exactly one PSC-consistent trajectory,
non-computably but lawfully.

\paragraph{Bohmian mechanics / de Broglie--Bohm.}
Bohmian mechanics~\cite{Bohm1952} assigns particles definite positions guided
by a pilot wave; the theory is explicitly non-local.
In GTE, the \fmdl{} orbit is the deterministic trajectory (deterministic CA
dynamics); the pilot-wave role is played by the PSC-admissible sector of
$\PHIMDL$.
GTE is local (AFCA causal invariance); Bohm is not.

\paragraph{Objective collapse / GRW.}
GRW-type theories~\cite{GhirardiRiminiWeber1986} postulate physical wavefunction
collapse triggered by a stochastic process.
In GTE, transputation is a physical collapse-like process, but forced by PSC
(non-algorithmic, lawful) rather than postulated.
The discrete mechanism ($p \to \fmdl{}$ orbit) is GTE's derived collapse.

\paragraph{Decoherence approaches.}
Decoherence~\cite{Zurek2003} explains apparent classicality and selects a
preferred basis, but does not resolve the measurement problem: the Born rule
remains an external postulate.
In GTE, the Born rule is derived (four routes, \CatAL).
Decoherence corresponds to the Level~0 chaotic dynamics of $p$; the transition
to \fmdl{} dynamics is the decoherence event that resolves outcomes.

\paragraph{GTE's position.}
Transputation is the fourth option established in P48~\cite{SpivackGTECompleteFramework}:
(a)~lawful ($D_1$--$D_2$), (b)~non-algorithmic ($D_3$, forced by the Physical
Incompleteness Theorem), (c)~PSC-consistent ($D_4$), and (d)~Born-consistent
($D_5$).
It differs from collapse (no external rule), many-worlds (selects, not
branches), Bohm (local, not guided), and GRW (derived, not postulated).

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=red!4,colframe=red!40,title={What Transputation Is Not},
  fonttitle=\bfseries,breakable]
\small
Transputation (the GTE measurement mechanism) is \emph{not}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Copenhagen collapse:} no external classical apparatus is invoked; the quantum-classical
    boundary IS the Level~0$\to$1 transition ($p \to \fmdl{}$).
  \item \textbf{Many-worlds branching:} exactly one PSC-consistent trajectory is realized, not all.
  \item \textbf{Bohmian pilot wave:} GTE is local (AFCA causal invariance); no non-local hidden variable.
  \item \textbf{GRW objective collapse:} transputation is derived from PSC, not postulated as an additional law.
  \item \textbf{Decoherence alone:} decoherence selects a preferred basis but does not resolve outcomes;
    transputation resolves outcomes.
  \item \textbf{Hypercomputation:} the adjudication function on the diagonal fragment has Turing degree
    exactly $\mathbf{0}'$ and delivers no certified $\Sigma^0_1$ decisions at finite stages;
    physics never requires oracle access above the first jump.\\
    (Theorems~\ref{thm:degree_complete} and~\ref{thm:no_modulus})
\end{itemize}
Transputation IS: the unique PSC-consistent, non-computable, lawful internal adjudicator forced by
