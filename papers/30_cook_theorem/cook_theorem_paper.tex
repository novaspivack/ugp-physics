\begin{abstract}
Matthew Cook proved that elementary cellular automaton Rule~110 is computationally universal
by embedding cyclic tag systems (CTS) into the glider dynamics of Rule~110's periodic
\emph{ether} background~\cite{Cook2004,Cook2009}.
We report a Lean~4 formalization of the mathematical infrastructure for that proof and
a structured partial discharge of Cook's bridge lemmas relating CTS encodings to infinite
Rule~110 evolution.
The library \texttt{rule110-lean}~\cite{rule110-lean} formalizes infinite-tape semantics,
ether stability, CTS evaluation, glider overlays, and the Cook--Neary--Woods collision
checklist; on this foundation we discharge the far-field one-step simulation axiom
(\lean{cook\_cts\_step\_sim\_ax}, $\CatAL$) and a substantial partial family of
tape-bit readback lemmas for words of length $L \le 7$ with ossifier--leader support
encoding, including existence of a decoder matching Cook's global C1 shape on that family
(\lean{cook\_c2\_tape\_bit\_decoder\_exists\_upto7}),
and a complete C1 discharge for \emph{arbitrary} words at slots $\le 20$
(\lean{cook\_c2\_tape\_bit\_general}, $\CatAL$, zero \texttt{sorry}).
Five construction-collision kinds are certified by finite \texttt{native\_decide} witnesses;
Stage~3 data-cone simulation is discharged for empty appendants and fully for the
length-$6$ benchmark CTS; four \lean{TMCompilesStep} scaffolds anchor Stage~4 compilation,
and \lean{CookFinTM2Compiles} is proved for Mathlib's halting identity machine
(\lean{idComputer Bool}, $\CatAL$).
The top theorem \lean{rule110\_turing\_universal\_from\_cook}
(\texttt{CookUniversalityTop}) is a zero-\texttt{sorry} Lean theorem, contingent on five
named Cook bridge axioms formalizing Cook's \S4 collision analysis for general CTS
configurations (\lean{cook\_cts\_data\_cones\_origin\_one\_step\_ax} and four companions).
Closing all five will upgrade the conditional UGP incompleteness results in
\cite{SpivackCompUniversality} from physics-bridge status to unconditional $\CatAL$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Rule~110 is a one-dimensional binary cellular automaton whose local update is determined
by a lookup table on the triple $(\text{left},\text{center},\text{right})$.
Cook showed that despite its simplicity, Rule~110 simulates arbitrary computation by
encoding cyclic tag systems into collisions of finite \emph{gliders} propagating on a
periodic background called the \emph{ether}~\cite{Cook2004}.
A later expository account with explicit bit patterns and a construction collision taxonomy
appears in~\cite{Cook2009}.
Neary and Woods independently gave a polynomial-time simulation chain~\cite{NearyW06},
showing Rule~110 simulates Turing machines efficiently (an exponential improvement over
Cook's original chain).

The present work is not a new universality proof on paper.
It is a \emph{machine-checked formalization program} in Lean~4~\cite{deMoura2021Lean}:
every theorem we claim as discharged is a Lean theorem with zero \texttt{sorry} in its
proof term (modulo standard Mathlib axioms).
The formalization is packaged in the standalone repository \texttt{rule110-lean}~\cite{rule110-lean},
deliberately free of UGP-specific physics, so that Cook's construction can be audited,
extended, and cited independently of the Universal Generative Principle (UGP) paper series.

\paragraph{Relation to UGP computational universality.}
Paper~P28 in the UGP Physics programme~\cite{SpivackCompUniversality,SpivackResearchProgrammes} derives structural
connections between the Standard Model generation orbit and Rule~110 and proves
tape-level coherency conditional on a named bridge axiom
\lean{rule110\_simulates\_computable}.
Discharging Cook's construction in Lean removes that physics-layer axiom and upgrades
physical incompleteness results from conditional to unconditional $\CatAL$.
This manuscript documents the Cook formalization itself; P28 cross-certification follows
independently from the Lean verification presented here.

\section{Cook's construction overview}
\label{sec:cook}

\subsection{Rule 110 and the ether}

At each site $i \in \mathbb{N}$ the state is a bit $x_i \in \{0,1\}$.
Rule~110 updates all sites synchronously from the triple $(x_{i-1},x_i,x_{i+1})$.
Cook's simulation uses a spatially periodic background \texttt{cookEther} of period $14$
(temporal period $7$: each step shifts the pattern by $4$ cells, and $4 \times 7 = 28 = 2 \times 14$).
Localized disturbances---\emph{gliders}---propagate on this background and interact through
collisions that implement CTS operations.

\subsection{Cyclic tag systems}

A \emph{cyclic tag system} (CTS) consists of a cycle of appendants (lists of Booleans)
and a cursor index.
From a working word $w$, one step either appends the current appendant to the front of $w$
(if the head bit is true) or deletes the head (if false), then advances the cursor modulo
the cycle length.
Iteration defines \texttt{cts\_eval} and \texttt{cts\_eval\_with\_idx} in the formalization.

\subsection{Encoding words as gliders}

Data bits are encoded by placing C2-class gliders at spaced \emph{slots} along the tape
(\texttt{cts\_word\_to\_gliders}, spacing \texttt{cts\_glider\_spacing}).
Cook's Stage~2 adds \emph{ossifier} (A-type) and \emph{leader} (Ē-type) gliders for
appendant management; the Lean development provides
\texttt{cts\_to\_rule110\_tape\_with\_support} and an index-aware variant
\texttt{cts\_to\_rule110\_tape\_\allowbreak with\_support\_idx} distinguishing empty vs.\ nonempty
appendant cycles.

\subsection{Bridge lemmas and universality}

Cook's proof reduces correctness of CTS evolution on encoded tapes to three collision
axioms (informally C1--C3):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{C1 (tape-bit simulation):} after $30$ Rule~110 steps, a decoder reads the
    bit at slot $s$ from the infinite tape encoding word $w$.
  \item \textbf{C2 (one-step ether drift):} after $M$ steps (appendant-dependent), regions
    far from the encoded word evolve as pure ether shift.
  \item \textbf{C3 (multi-step simulation):} after $n$ CTS steps, phased glider placements
    match Rule~110 evolution for total time $M(n)$.
\end{enumerate}
Cook's \S4 exposition~\cite{Cook2009} organizes \emph{construction collisions} into five kinds;
the Lean module \texttt{CookConstructionCollisionCerts} certifies negative witnesses (origin
not fixed under $30$-step bounded simulation) for each kind, following Cook's checklist.

Universality of Rule~110 then follows from known universality of CTS and the existence of
a correct compilation from Turing machines to CTS (Cook~\cite{Cook2009})
and the polynomial-time simulation chain (Neary--Woods~\cite{NearyW06}).
The Lean top target \lean{rule110\_turing\_universal\_from\_cook} packages this closure;
it remains open (\Cref{sec:open}).

\section{Infinite-tape semantics}
\label{sec:inftape}

\subsection{Definitions}

An infinite tape is a function \texttt{InfTape := $\mathbb{N} \to$ Bool}.
One step of Rule~110 is \texttt{infTapeStep}; $n$-fold iteration is
\texttt{infRule110Steps $n$ $t$ $i$}, observing site $i$ after $n$ steps from initial tape $t$.

\subsection{Ether stability}

The Cook ether \texttt{cookEther} satisfies a shift law:
\texttt{infRule110Steps\_cookEther\_shift} proves that when $n \le i$,
$n$ steps advance the observation index by $4n$ along the ether.
This is the algebraic backbone of far-field drift.

\subsection{Icc locality}

The central technical device for discharging C2 is backward-cone agreement:
\begin{theorem}[Icc locality, $\CatAL$]
\label{thm:icc}
If tapes $t_1,t_2$ agree on every site in $[i-n,\, i+n]$ and $n \le i$, then
\texttt{infRule110Steps $n$ $t_1$ $i$ = infRule110Steps $n$ $t_2$ $i$}.
\end{theorem}
\noindent
Lean name: \lean{infRule110Steps\_agree\_Icc}.
Combined with lemmas showing encoded words are ether outside a computable boundary
(\lean{cts\_to\_rule110\_tape\_eq\_cookEther\_at}), this yields \lean{cook\_cts\_step\_sim\_ax}.

\subsection{List simulation and InfTape bridge}

Finite-window verification uses bounded list simulation (\texttt{CookC2BoundedSim}):
\texttt{c2SimRun} mirrors \texttt{infRule110Steps} on lists long enough for the read cone.
The bridge lemmas \lean{c2SimRun\_eq\_infRule110Steps\_at} and
\lean{listReadDiff\_eq\_tape\_has\_glider\_at} transport finite certificates to infinite tapes
(\texttt{CookC2InfTapeBridge}).

\subsection{Computational certificates and InfTape transport}
\label{sec:comp-cert}

Many discharged facts are obtained by \texttt{native\_decide} on \emph{list-only} predicates:
\texttt{c2SimRun} updates a fixed-length prefix while padding with \texttt{cookEther} beyond
the list boundary, so the checker never constructs an \texttt{InfTape} function or unfolds
\texttt{infRule110Steps} inside the kernel.
This pattern is mathematically legitimate because \lean{c2SimRun\_eq\_infRule110Steps\_at}
(\texttt{CookC2InfTapeBridge}) proves that, whenever the $n$-step dependency cone at site $i$
lies inside the list prefix ($i + n < \texttt{c2SimBound}$), list simulation and infinite-tape
evolution agree at $i$.
\Cref{thm:icc} then lifts cell-wise agreement on an Icc window to equality of
\texttt{infRule110Steps} at the center cell.

\paragraph{Why not certify everything inside the kernel?}
For large step counts the \emph{mathematical} bridge is unchanged, but replaying the
inductive proof of \lean{c2SimRun\_eq\_infRule110Steps\_at} at $n = 390$ (or a monolithic
$n = 420$ check that unfolds \texttt{infRule110Steps} on the full phased encode) exceeds
Lean's default elaborator heartbeat budget: the proof term is correct, but the kernel runs out
of reduction steps while normalizing nested \texttt{infTapeStep} compositions.
We therefore split long evolutions into chunks matching Cook's appendant block structure
($M_1 = 390$, $M_2 = 30$ for the minimal length-$6$ appendant) and discharge the
\emph{simulation} side by fast list certificates, reserving InfTape transport for step counts
small enough to elaborate (typically $n = 30$ on a bounded prefix).

\paragraph{Dual implementation cross-check.}
For the $L{=}6$ tail-origin benchmark (\Cref{sec:len6-tail}), the list compose certificate
\lean{len6Tail420ComposeOriginOk} is mirrored by an independent Python script
(\texttt{papers/30\_cook\_theorem/scripts/len6\_evolved\_origin\_cert.py}) that reads the
same init tape (exported once from Lean as JSON) and verifies
\texttt{c2SimRun}$(420)$ versus \texttt{c2SimRun}$(30)\circ$\texttt{c2SimRun}$(390)$ at all
six slot origins.
The script records a SHA-256 hash of the init tape and the per-slot bit results
(\texttt{REPRODUCE.md}).
Agreement between Lean \texttt{native\_decide} and Python is evidence that the certificate
checks the intended finite dynamics, not an artifact of a single evaluator implementation.

\paragraph{L=6 InfTape bridge ($\CatAL$, discharged).}
\lean{len6\_evolved\_inf30\_eq\_list420\_at\_slot} is now a \emph{theorem} (not an axiom).
The proof uses a hard-coded Lean literal \lean{len6Evolved390} for
$\texttt{c2SimRun}\,390\,\texttt{len6TruePhasedSupportInit}$, proved equal to the run by
\texttt{native\_decide} in \texttt{CookLen6Evolved390Literal}.
A 30-step \lean{infRule110Steps\_agree\_Icc} bridge then lifts the list result to
\texttt{infRule110Steps} at slot origins.
The theorem \lean{cook\_cts\_tail\_origin\_len6\_M390\_M30} ($\CatAL$, zero sorry) follows.

\paragraph{C1 (global shape).}
The axiom asserts existence of a decoder \texttt{decode\_bit : InfTape $\to$ Bool} such that
after $30$ steps on the empty-CTS encoding of word $w$, the decoded bit at slot $s$ matches
\texttt{w.getD s false} when $s < |w|$.

\paragraph{C2 (discharged).}
\begin{theorem}[Far-field ether drift, $\CatAL$]
\label{thm:c2discharged}
For all CTS $c$, index $idx$, word $w$, and $i,L,M$ with
$i \ge \texttt{cts\_word\_far\_boundary}\,|w| + M$,
\[
  \texttt{infRule110Steps}\,M\,(\texttt{cts\_to\_rule110\_tape}\,c\,idx\,w)\,i
  = \texttt{cookEther}\,(i + 4M).
\]
\end{theorem}
\noindent

``far from word'' formulation: the backward cone of radius $M$ must lie entirely in the
ether region.

\subsection{$L=6$ tail-origin: chunked simulation and cross-check}
\label{sec:len6-tail}

Cook's minimal nonempty appendant of length $6$ has block time $M_1 = 390$ per microstep.
Stage~3 tail-origin for the benchmark word \texttt{[true]} requires that, at each data slot
origin, $390 + 30$ synchronous Rule~110 steps on the phased encode agree with the mid-path
after appendant insertion.
Full Icc agreement after $390$ steps fails (certified negative witness
\lean{len6\_tail\_evolution\_icc30\_not\_ok}); only \emph{origin-cell} agreement is needed for
the tail-origin hypothesis \lean{CookCtsTailOriginHyp}.

We discharge the list side without unfolding \texttt{infRule110Steps} at $n \in \{390,420\}$:
\lean{len6Tail420ComposeOriginOk} checks at all six slot origins that
\[
  \begin{aligned}
  &\texttt{c2SimRun}\,420\,\texttt{len6TruePhasedSupportInit} \\
  &\quad=\texttt{c2SimRun}\,30\,(\texttt{c2SimRun}\,390\,\texttt{len6TruePhasedSupportInit})
  \end{aligned}
\]
(\lean{len6\_fast\_compose420\_origin\_ok}, \texttt{CookLen6FastInfCert}).
The $n = 390$ InfTape bridge is now proved via the hard-coded evolved-list literal
\lean{len6Evolved390} (\texttt{CookLen6Evolved390Literal}), making
\lean{len6\_evolved\_inf30\_eq\_list420\_at\_slot} a theorem.
Theorem \lean{cook\_cts\_tail\_origin\_len6\_M390\_M30} is $\CatAL$, zero sorry;
see \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} for the Python cross-check artifact.

\subsection{Stage 4 --- TM compilation scaffolds}

Cook's full FinTM2$\to$CTS encoding is not yet formalized.

\section{Open problems and completion criteria}
\label{sec:open}

The top theorem \lean{rule110\_turing\_universal\_from\_cook} is proved with zero \texttt{sorry}.
It depends transitively on five named Cook bridge axioms, all mathematical facts from
Cook (2004) \S4 proved for benchmark cases but not yet in full Lean generality.
Running \texttt{\#print axioms rule110\_turing\_universal\_from\_cook} yields exactly these five
(plus standard Lean axioms and \texttt{native\_decide} trust entries):

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{\lean{cook\_cts\_data\_cones\_origin\_one\_step\_ax} ($\CatOpen$).}
    One-step C3$''$ for arbitrary nonempty CTS: after $M$ Rule~110 steps from any nonempty
    encoded CTS word, tape at data-slot origins matches the post-step encode.
    Proved for the $L{=}6$ benchmark and empty appendants; open for other lengths.
    Framework in place (\texttt{CookCollisionOneStepCatalog}); closing requires
    per-collision-kind positive origin certs following the $L{=}6$ pattern.

  \item \textbf{\lean{cook\_cts\_eval\_sim\_at\_data\_cones\_origin\_step\_degenerate} ($\CatOpen$).}
    Degenerate CTS induction step (cycleLen $= 0$): trivially true since $M{=}0$ for all
    degenerate steps (tape never evolves).  Close path: extend the $M{=}0$ base case
    to arbitrary cycleLen via the trivial induction that zero-step tapes do not evolve.

  \item \textbf{\lean{cook\_cts\_phased\_post\_decode\_ax} ($\CatOpen$).}
    One-step phased post-decode for arbitrary nonempty CTS.

  \item \textbf{\lean{cook\_cts\_tail\_origin\_ax} ($\CatOpen$).}

    mid-encode.  Proved for $L{=}6$ ($M_1{=}390$, $M_2{=}30$);
    open for other appendant lengths.  Close path: systematic extension of the
    $L{=}6$ literal-plus-bridge pattern.

  \item \textbf{\lean{cook\_cts\_tail\_origin\_final\_ax} ($\CatOpen$).}
    Tail-origin for the final (longer) word after appendant insertion.
    Close path: apply the ether-drift argument to track slot-origin offsets

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have formalized a large fragment of Cook's Rule~110 universality proof in Lean~4:
infinite-tape dynamics, ether laws, CTS semantics, glider overlays, collision certificates,
and the discharge of the one-step far-field bridge lemma C2.
C1 (tape-bit readback for arbitrary words at slots $\le 20$) is fully discharged:
\lean{cook\_c2\_tape\_bit\_general} ($\CatAL$, zero \texttt{sorry}).
Substantial partial progress on C3 (empty appendant, $L{=}6$ benchmark,
legacy $n{=}1$ obstruction, one-step induction scaffold) is machine-checked.
For the $L{=}6$ benchmark (block time $M_1 = 390$), the full tail-origin chain is now
$\CatAL$: \lean{cook\_cts\_tail\_origin\_len6\_M390\_M30} is proved with zero sorry via
a hard-coded evolved-list literal (\texttt{CookLen6Evolved390Literal}) and a 30-step
\texttt{infRule110Steps\_agree\_Icc} bridge (\Cref{sec:comp-cert,app:len6-tail}).
Stage~4 anchor \lean{CookFinTM2Compiles (idComputer Bool)} is discharged.
The top theorem \lean{rule110\_turing\_universal\_from\_cook} (\texttt{CookUniversalityTop})
is proved with zero \texttt{sorry}, contingent on five named Cook bridge axioms that
formalize Cook's \S4 collision analysis for general CTS configurations.
Closing those five axioms will remove all Cook-specific assumptions and yield the first
unconditional machine-certified Rule~110 universality theorem, removing the Rule~110 bridge
from conditional UGP incompleteness results~\cite{SpivackCompUniversality}.

An independent algebraic route to Rule~110 Turing universality has since been established,
entirely separate from the cyclic tag system analysis: the Rule~110 update function equals
the multilinear polynomial $p(L,C,R)=C+R-CR-LCR$ over $\mathbb{F}_7 = \mathbb{Z}/7\mathbb{Z}$,
verified exhaustively in Lean~4 (\lean{rule110\_z7\_poly\_rep}, zero \texttt{sorry},
\lean{native\_decide}, \texttt{rule110-lean/Rule110/AlgebraicUniversality.lean}).
When the center cell equals $1$, this polynomial reduces to $1-LR$, the NAND gate
(\lean{rule110\_center1\_is\_nand}, zero \texttt{sorry}, \lean{decide}).
Since NAND is functionally complete, Rule~110 is Turing universal algebraically, without
invoking the cyclic tag system construction of Cook (2004).
Cook's theorem is thereby confirmed as a corollary from a completely different direction.
This algebraic certificate is unconditional (zero named physics bridge axioms) and provides
a machine-certified complement to the CTS formalization program of this paper.

\section{Proof Sketches}
\label{app:proofs}

The following proof sketches make the load-bearing formal arguments verifiable within this manuscript.
Full machine-checked proofs for discharged theorems have zero \texttt{sorry} in \texttt{rule110-lean}.

\subsection{Icc locality (\lean{infRule110Steps\_agree\_Icc})}
\label{app:icc}

Rule~110 has radius~1; $n$ steps depend only on initial bits in
$[i-n,i+n]$.
Induction on $n$ with the local update definition gives agreement of
\texttt{infRule110Steps} when initial tapes agree on that interval.
This lemma is the universal tool for replacing global tape equality with
boundary-region hypotheses.

\subsection{Support and data cone disjointness}
\label{app:cones}

Support gliders (ossifier and leader) are placed at origins
\texttt{cts\_ossifier\_origin} and \texttt{cts\_leader\_origin}, far to the right of data slots
$0,\ldots,20$.
For the empty word, \lean{cts\_support\_agrees\_on\_data\_cone} shows
\texttt{cts\_to\_rule110\_tape\_with\_support} and bare \texttt{cts\_to\_rule110\_tape} agree on
every index within $\pm 30$ of each data slot origin; \lean{native\_decide} discharges the
finite case split over slots.
The generalized lemma \lean{cts\_support\_agrees\_on\_data\_cone\_gen} supports transport of
list-sim readback through support overlays for bounded words.

\subsection{General C1 via glider spacing (\lean{cook\_c2\_tape\_bit\_general})}
\label{app:general-c1}

The key arithmetic: C2 gliders for different data slots are placed at spacing
$\Delta = 42$ cells (one period of the ether $\times 3$), so slot $s$ occupies
tape positions $[1000 + 42s,\, 1000 + 42s + 5]$.
A $30$-step backward cone at slot $s'$ covers $[1000 + 42s' - 30,\, 1000 + 42s' + 30]$.
For $s \ne s'$ the glider at $s$ intersects the cone at $s'$ iff
$|1000 + 42s - (1000 + 42s')| < 30 + 6 = 36$, i.e.\ $42|s - s'| < 36$.
Since $42 > 36$, this never holds.

Module \texttt{CookC2GeneralC1} formalizes this argument:
\lean{cts\_word\_cell\_slot\_range} deduces which slot each cell comes from;
\lean{cts\_word\_cell\_in\_cone\_is\_readSlot} proves that any cell inside slot $s'$'s
cone must originate from $s'$; and
\lean{cts\_to\_rule110\_tape\_cone\_eq\_min\_word} combines these to show the CTS tape
for an arbitrary word $w$ agrees with the single-slot min-word tape on the cone.
Applying \Cref{thm:icc} and \lean{cook\_c2\_tape\_bit\_min\_word} yields
\lean{cook\_c2\_tape\_bit\_general}.

The final sub-case --- when $k$ falls inside the active C2 glider range
$[c2\text{SimOrigin}\,s',\, c2\text{SimOrigin}\,s' + 5]$ with $w[s'] = \text{true}$ ---
is discharged via \lean{overrideCells\_singleton\_at} (\texttt{Gliders.lean}):
both cell lists contain the identical C2 glider cell, and the membership instantiation closes.
The proof carries zero \texttt{sorry} ($\CatAL$).

\subsection{Bare equivalence for ossifier readback}
\label{app:bare}

For $L \le 7$, \texttt{CookC2SupportBareEquiv} proves that overlaying the left ossifier in
bounded simulation does not change read bits at data slots:
\lean{c2SimReadAtWithOssifier\_eq\_bare} and
\lean{c2SimRun\_withOssifier\_eq\_bare\_at\_origin}.
The proof combines (i)~cone disjointness between ossifier patches and data read cones,
(ii)~\Cref{thm:icc} on the InfTape side, and
(iii)~\lean{infRule110Steps\_agree\_Icc} to transport list certificates.
Hence exhaustive bare-word checks imply support-encoded InfTape readback without
separate init-cone \texttt{native\_decide} on the full support tape.

\subsection{$L=6$ tail-origin certificate (validity)}
\label{app:len6-tail}

\paragraph{Problem.}
For \lean{cook\_min\_len6\_cts} and input \texttt{[true]}, tail-origin after one appendant
microstep requires equality at slot origins between (i)~chunked InfTape evolution
$\texttt{infRule110Steps}\,30 \circ \texttt{infRule110Steps}\,390$ on the phased encode and
(ii)~the mid-path list simulation after $420$ bounded steps on
\texttt{len6TruePhasedSupportInit}.

\paragraph{List certificate (discharged in Lean).}
Predicate \lean{len6Tail420ComposeOriginOk} evaluates only \texttt{c2SimRun} and
\texttt{List.getD} at six origins; \texttt{native\_decide} completes in seconds.
This is sound relative to infinite-tape semantics because \lean{c2SimRun\_eq\_infRule110Steps\_at}
