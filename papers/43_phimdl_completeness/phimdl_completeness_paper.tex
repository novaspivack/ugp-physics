\paragraph{Kink mass and $T_{\mu\nu}$.}
The BPS kink profile $\Phi_{\rm kink}(x) = (4/7)\arctan\exp(m_\varphi x)$
was numerically integrated by midpoint rule ($10^5$--$2\times10^5$ points over
$|x| \leq 25/m_\varphi$).  The stress tensor components were evaluated by
second-order finite differences with double-precision arithmetic.
Results: $\int T_{00}\,dx = 290.10$~MeV (relative error $1.4\times10^{-6}$);
conservation check: $\max|\partial^\mu T_{\mu\nu}| = 6\times10^{-12}$
(scripts: \texttt{papers/38\_emergent\_gravity\_phimdl/scripts/phimdl\_tmunu\_full.py},
\texttt{phimdl\_kink\_gravitational\_source.py}).

\paragraph{Lyapunov exponent.}
The 3+1D $f_{\rm MDL}$ Lyapunov exponent was computed over all $7^6 = 117{,}649$
6-neighbour configurations plus structured high-entropy seeds (total
$823{,}543$ inputs) using the standard one-step perturbation method.
Result: $\lambda = 0.8575 \approx 6/7$ (uniform output distribution over
$\{1,\ldots,6\}$).

refined scan at 105 trials around $\lambda_c = 0.54\pm0.04$).

Result: 0 Class~4 hits; sharp chaos transition at $\lambda_c\approx0.54\pm0.04$.
