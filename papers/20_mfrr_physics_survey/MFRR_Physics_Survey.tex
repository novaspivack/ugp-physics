\section{Introduction and Scope}
\label{sec:intro}
%------------------------------------------------------------------

The Mathematical Foundations of Reflexive Reality (MFRR) programme~\cite{SpivackMFRR}
asks a question of logical necessity: what mathematical structure is forced upon a universe
that is required to be Perfectly Self-Contained?
A universe is \emph{Perfectly Self-Contained} (PSC) if its laws are not imposed externally
but are executed internally, energetically self-accounting, and logically closed.
Three independent routes force this requirement:

\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Logical (G\"odel--Turing):} Self-referential systems cannot have external meta-laws without infinite regress~\cite{Goedel1931,Turing1936}.
\item \textbf{Energetic (Landauer):} An external law requires infinite energy to execute; the Reflexive Landauer Bound closes this gap~\cite{Landauer1961}.
  \item \textbf{Geometric (Norfleet):} Non-zero holonomy defect $\delta = \Lambda - \pi/12 \neq 0$ prevents global potential existence, forcing local irreversible choices~\cite{NorfleetPrivComm}.%
  \footnote{The symbol $\Lambda$ here denotes Norfleet's dimensionless
    Information Profit Threshold constant $\Lambda_{\rm IPT} = \ln\varphi/\ln(2\pi)
    \approx 0.26177$, \emph{not} the cosmological constant.  The holonomy defect
    $\delta = \Lambda_{\rm IPT} - \pi/12 \approx +3.09\times10^{-5}$ has no direct
    connection to the cosmological constant problem.}
\end{enumerate}

The central construct realizing PSC is \emph{Transputation} ($\PT$) ---
lawful internal adjudication of computational degeneracies at Choice Points:
\[
\PT(\theta^\star, \mathcal{B}) =
\arg\min_{\gamma_i \in \mathcal{B}} \bigl[ D(\gamma_i) + \lambda\, C(\gamma_i) \bigr],
\]
where the universe selects among admissible branches by minimizing ontological dissonance $D$
plus informational cost $C$.
The No-Emulation Theorem \tagT{} (\texttt{NemS.no\_emulation\_thm}) proves $\PT$ cannot
be simulated by any Turing machine; the Forced Adjudication theorem \tagT{}
(\texttt{closed\_choice\_forces\_transputation}, NEMS 08, DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19429882}{10.5281/zenodo.19429882})
establishes $\PT$ as the \emph{unique} internal adjudicator under closed-choice conditions.

This paper surveys the formal and computational results of the MFRR programme at a level
suitable for journal submission.
The full proofs and computational experiments are documented in the
15,185-line MFRR monograph~\cite{SpivackMFRR}, the zero-sorry companion Lean~4 library~\cite{SpivackUGPFormalization,ugp-lean}
(Lean 4.29.0-rc6 / Mathlib 4.29.0-rc6),
and the companion papers on SM structure~\cite{Spivack2026_SM_UGP},
