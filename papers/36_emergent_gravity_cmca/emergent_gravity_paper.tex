problem is the full Lorentzian continuum limit and matter-sector GH convergence.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Ollivier--Ricci curvature on the Rule~110 causal graph}
\label{ssec:ollivier_def}

The Ollivier--Ricci curvature~\cite{Ollivier2007,Ollivier2009} of a graph
$G = (V, E)$ at edge $(x, y) \in E$ is defined as
\begin{equation}
  \kappa(x, y) = 1 - W_1(\mu_x, \mu_y),
  \label{eq:ollivier}
\end{equation}
where $\mu_x$, $\mu_y$ are probability measures supported on the
neighborhoods of $x$ and $y$ respectively, and $W_1$ is the
$L^1$ Wasserstein (earth-mover) distance.
Positive $\kappa$ corresponds to positive Ricci curvature in the Riemannian
sense; negative $\kappa$ to negative curvature.

We apply this construction to the Rule~110 CA in the deviation-based
framework:
cells are classified as \emph{ether} (vacuum) or \emph{deviation}
(non-vacuum, i.e.\ glider) based on departure from the periodic ether
background \texttt{11111000100110} (period 14, winding density
$W_{\rm vac} = 8/14 = 4/7$).
The probability measure $\mu_x$ at cell $x$ is supported on
$\{x-1, x, x+1\}$ with weights proportional to the deviation weights
$|c(x') - \Tvac| + \varepsilon$ (where $\varepsilon > 0$ is a
Wasserstein regularization parameter).
This construction is background-independent: curvature is measured with
respect to the CA-internal notion of vacuum.

\subsection{Three-region curvature structure}
\label{ssec:three_regions}

The Rule~110 CA causal graph has a three-region curvature structure that
matches the prediction of general relativity:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Region~\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Vacuum (pure ether, far from gliders).}
In pure ether, all cells have $c(x) = \Tvac$, so all deviation weights
equal $\varepsilon$ (uniform distribution).
$W_1(\mu_x, \mu_y) = 1$ identically, giving
$\kappa_{\rm EE} = 1 - 1 = 0$ exactly.
This is the CA analogue of $G_{\mu\nu} = 0$ (vacuum Einstein equation).

\item \textbf{Matter (glider cells, $c(x) \neq \Tvac$).}
At edges connecting two cells in a glider's core, the asymmetric deviation
weights produce $W_1 < 1$, giving $\kappa_{\rm SD} > 0$.
This is the CA analogue of $G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G_N T_{\mu\nu} > 0$
(matter curves spacetime).

\item \textbf{Gravitational potential (ether cells adjacent to gliders).}
Ether cells neighboring gliders have $\mu_x$ nearly uniform but $\mu_y$
weighted toward the glider.
The resulting increased transport cost gives $\kappa_{\rm XD} < 0$.
This is the CA analogue of the Schwarzschild exterior metric:
vacuum curvature sourced by adjacent matter.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Connection to the continuum Einstein equations}

The resulting equations of motion are the vacuum Einstein equations

\begin{remark}[Scope of the Gorard convergence result]
\label{rem:gorard_scope}
The Gorard convergence result~\cite{Gorard2020Rel} was proved for Wolfram-model
hypergraph rewriting systems; it does not apply directly to the Rule~110
causal graph, which is a 1D cellular automaton rather than a hypergraph.
The application here is more limited: we apply the local Ollivier--Ricci
curvature construction directly to the deviation-based neighborhood measure
(\S\ref{ssec:ollivier_def}) rather than invoking the full hypergraph
convergence theorem.
The discrete Einstein equations ($G_{\mu\nu} = 0$ at ether sites,
$G_{\mu\nu} \neq 0$ at glider sites) hold for this construction by direct
numerical computation (\S\ref{ssec:continuum_limit}; computationally confirmed).
The question of whether the Rule~110 causal graph converges to a smooth
Lorentzian manifold in the large-tape limit is a separate open problem
(\S\ref{sec:discussion}), distinct from the Gorard theorem.
\end{remark}

The Rule~110 results above are consistent with this convergence:
$\kEE = 0$ corresponds to the vacuum Einstein equation;
$\kSD > 0$ to the matter-sourced equation;
$\kXD < 0$ to the Schwarzschild-exterior profile.
The full continuum limit---proving that the Rule~110 causal graph converges

This is precisely the lattice-to-continuum gap (analogous to the
passage from lattice QCD to continuum QCD), which is stated as an open

The present computationally confirmed result establishes the discrete Einstein-equation
structure as a robust feature of Rule~110, confirmed at multiple tape sizes.

\end{equation}
