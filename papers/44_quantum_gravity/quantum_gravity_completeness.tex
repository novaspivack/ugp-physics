\subsection{The Lagrangian $\LL[\PHIMDL;\,g_{\mu\nu}]$}
\label{sec:lagrangian}

The most general Lorentz-covariant, $\ZS$-symmetric Lagrangian for a real scalar field
$\PHIMDL$ on a curved background takes the form
\begin{equation}
  \LL[\PHIMDL;\,g_{\mu\nu}] = \sqrt{|g|}\Bigl[
    \tfrac{1}{2}g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\PHIMDL\,\partial_\nu\PHIMDL
    - V_{\ZS}(\PHIMDL) - \tfrac{1}{2}\xi R\,\PHIMDL^2
    \Bigr] + \LL_{\rm EH},
  \label{eq:L_general}
\end{equation}
where $V_{\ZS}(\Phi) = (m^2/49)(1-\cos 7\Phi)$ is the $\ZS$-periodic sine-Gordon
potential, $\xi$ is the non-minimal coupling, and $\LL_{\rm EH} = \sqrt{|g|}R/(16\pi G)$
is the Einstein-Hilbert action.  Three independent arguments force $\xi=0$:

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Argument 1 (MDL minimality).}  The Minimum Description Length
principle selects the Lagrangian with the fewest free parameters.  The non-minimal
coupling $\xi$ is a free parameter in~\eqref{eq:L_general} with no constraint from the
flat-background $\PHIMDL$ theory (established in P43~\cite{SpivackCompleteness}),
where $\xi$ is not present.  MDL minimality therefore sets $\xi = 0$ as the
minimum-complexity choice.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Argument 2 (Wald entropy consistency).}  The Wald entropy
formula~\cite{Wald1993} for a theory with non-minimal coupling $\xi$ gives
\begin{equation}
  S_{\rm Wald}(\Gamma) = -2\pi\int_\Gamma \frac{\partial\LL}{\partial R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}}
  \,\hat{\epsilon}_{\mu\nu}\hat{\epsilon}_{\rho\sigma}\,dA.
\end{equation}
For $\xi\neq 0$, $\partial\LL/\partial R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$ receives a contribution
from the $\xi R\PHIMDL^2$ term that yields $S_{\rm Wald}\neq A/(4G)$ for surfaces where
$\PHIMDL\neq 0$.  Since the Bekenstein-Hawking formula $S_{\rm BH} = A/(4G)$ is
established from first principles in P38~\cite{SpivackPhiMDLGravity} and
P43~\cite{SpivackCompleteness}, consistency requires $\xi = 0$.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Argument 3 (Z$_7$-topology constraint).}  The $\ZS$-admissible
boundary conditions require the field to lie in a $\ZS$-bundle over the spacetime
manifold.  For $\pi_1(\mathcal{M})=0$ spacetimes, the bundle is topologically trivial and
the $\xi R\PHIMDL^2$ term vanishes for the vacuum sector $\PHIMDL = \Phi_k$; but for
non-trivial topologies (e.g., the Euclidean black hole), the curvature coupling would
break the $\ZS$-periodicity of the vacuum structure.  The unique coupling preserving
$\ZS$-topology on arbitrary backgrounds is $\xi=0$.

  Mathlib library infrastructure.

  self-consistency in the cosmological sector.
\end{itemize}
