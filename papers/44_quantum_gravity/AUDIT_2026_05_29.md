## A. Accuracy Issues Found and Fixed

### A1 — CRITICAL: n_s=1 tension stated as "4.3σ" — FIXED

- **Location:** §5.3, line 805 (original)
- **Issue:** The paper stated "The leading-order result $n_s = 1$ is in $4.3\sigma$ tension
  with the Planck~2018 measurement $n_s^{\rm obs} = 0.9649\pm 0.0042$." This is numerically
  wrong. Computation: $(1.000 - 0.9649)/0.0042 = 8.36\sigma$.
  
  The board value "minimum tension 4.3σ stands" refers to the tension between the
  *most optimistic GTE Bogoliubov estimate* ($n_s \geq 0.983$, LQC-scaling bound) and
  observation: $(0.983 - 0.9649)/0.0042 = 4.3\sigma$. It is a lower bound on the
  tension, not the tension of $n_s = 1$ vs observation.

- **Fix applied:** Changed to "~8.4σ tension" and added a sentence explicitly noting
  that even the most optimistic Bogoliubov bound ($n_s \geq 0.983$) remains at least
  4.3σ from observation.

### A2 — Theorem name mismatch in Remark 4.4 — FIXED

- **Location:** §4, Remark 4.4
- **Issue:** Remark 4.4 cited `ghet_t1_t2_t3_t4_t5_mutual_equivalence` as a Lean theorem,
  but this name does not exist in any form in ugp-lean or ugp-lean-exp. Appendix A lists
  the master GHET theorem as `ghet_all_20_implications`.
- **Fix applied:** Changed to `ghet_all_20_implications`, consistent with Appendix A.

### A3 — Lean bundle theorem name inconsistency — FIXED

- **Location:** §2.2 body text vs Appendix A
- **Issue:** Body text cited `phimdl_z7_bundle_trivial_on_simply_connected`; Appendix A
  listed `phimdl_z7_bundle_trivial`. Two different names for the same theorem.
- **Fix applied:** Unified to `phimdl_z7_bundle_trivial` (the Appendix A name).
