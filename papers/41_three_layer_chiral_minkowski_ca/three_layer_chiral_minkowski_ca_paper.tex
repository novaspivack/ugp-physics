\begin{abstract}
We derive physical observables from the three-layer Chiral Minkowski CA (CMCA):
Rule~110 ($L_{x^+}$, right-moving), Rule~124 ($L_{x^-}$, left-moving), and a
shared inner Rule~110 $\tau_c$ clock ($L_t$, timelike).

Four principal results:
(1) \textit{Lorentz invariance as observer effect:} PSC Presentation Invariance
forces all $[\mathbf{D}]$-selected observables to be Lorentz-equivariant on
the $\PHIMDL$ continuum; the lattice residual $\varepsilon_0(7) = \pi^2/147 \approx 6.71\%$
vanishes as $M^{-2}$ in the continuum limit; at LHC energies
$\delta_{\rm LV} \approx 8.8\times10^{-32}$ (\CatAL, zero sorry, one axiom).
(2) \textit{Electroweak scale:} $\sin^2\theta_W = 384729/1664000 \approx 0.231207$
($-0.42\sigma$ PDG~2022, \CatAL, Wolfenstein; two-loop correction: $+0.038\sigma$ PDG~2024, \CatB, P31~\cite{SpivackWeinbergAngle}); $M_Z = 91.91\,\text{GeV}$ ($+0.80\%$);
$M_W = 80.61\,\text{GeV}$ (CA-scale, $M{=}7$ resolution).
(3) \textit{Born rule:} $P(k) = |\phi_k|^2$ from canonical $\ZS$-KG kink
quantization (\lean{born\_rule\_unconditional}, zero sorry, zero custom physics axioms).
(4) \textit{Quantum interference:} the double-slit fringe pattern reproduced at
correlation $0.998$ vs Fraunhofer ($\chi^2_{\rm red} \approx 10^{-4}$).

The CMCA is the MDL-unique ($K_{\rm CA} = 19$ bits) Level-1 GTE model carrying
all five structural features: $\ZS$ generation orbit, V--A chirality, Turing
universality, observer-level Lorentz invariance, and SR proper-time dilation
(5.79\% error, the $M=7$ Nyquist floor).
The $3+1$D generalization is presented in~\cite{SpivackThreeTapeCMCA}.
\end{abstract}

This work continues the programme of digital physics initiated by

The Universal Generative Principle (\UGP{}) Physics programme, the quantitative branch of the Reflexive Reality programme~\cite{SpivackCompUniversality,SpivackResearchProgrammes}
derives Standard Model physics from first principles using a minimal discrete
arithmetic substrate.  The foundational object is the Generative Triple
Evolution (GTE) map, whose MDL-minimal realisation in one spatial dimension
is the cellular automaton rule f$_{\rm MDL}$---equivalent to Rule~110 on the
binary projection and a $\ZS$-valued update rule on the winding-number
projection~\cite{SpivackGTEMobius}.  Rule~110 is Turing-universal
(Cook~2004~\cite{Cook2004}; algebraically certified in~\cite{SpivackGF7Universality}).

A single-layer Rule~110 substrate reproduces many SM predictions:
the Weinberg angle~\cite{SpivackWeinbergAngle}, gauge group
structure~\cite{SpivackGTEUnification}, particle
masses~\cite{SpivackQMFromR110}, and the $\PHIMDL$ continuum
field~\cite{SpivackGTEMobius}.  However, a single chiral layer cannot
simultaneously account for all discrete symmetries of the Standard Model.
Specifically:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textit{Chiral symmetry (V--A):} the SM charged-current couples
    left-handed fermions only.  A single right-moving CA glider does not
    distinguish left- from right-handed currents.
  \item \textit{Lorentz invariance:} Rule~110 has a preferred direction
    (right-moving gliders travel at $v_R=+2/3$ in lattice units) and does
    not, by itself, exhibit the Lorentz boost symmetry of the continuum
    limit.
  \item \textit{Generation orbit asymmetry:} mirror decays proceed in 2 steps
    in a single chiral layer, while the forward orbit takes 3 steps.
\end{itemize}

These three requirements are resolved simultaneously by pairing Rule~110
with its \emph{spatial mirror}, Rule~124 (right-mirror: the left-moving
chiral layer).  The two-layer system---Rule~110 ($v_R=+2/3$) plus
Rule~124 ($v_L=-2/3$)---is the minimal substrate consistent with all SM
discrete symmetries.
The pair is itself arithmetically forced: over all $120$ family orderings
of the unordered UGP generation-orbit data, the admissible vacuum-transparent
survivor set is exactly $\{\text{Rule~110}, \text{Rule~124}\}$, with reversal
of the ordering bijecting the two (\lean{orbit\_chirality\_census},
machine-certified in Lean~4 with zero
sorry~\cite{SpivackGTEPolynomialWolfram,SpivackGTECompleteFramework}).
The two-layer chiral architecture thus instantiates the full survivor set,
and the choice of which member carries which direction is the chirality
gauge.

\paragraph{GTE substrate terminology.}
The GTE substrate is a triple $(A,\,e,\,[\mathbf{D}])$: $A$ is the cellular-automaton arithmetic layer; the full Chiral Minkowski CA $(A,e,[\mathbf{D}])$ triple is realized by Rule~110 ($L_{x^+}$) + Rule~124 ($L_{x^-}$) + inner Rule~110 $\tau_c$ clock ($L_t$); see Definition~\ref{def:cmca}.  $e$ is the evolutionary dynamics (the CA update rule $f_{\rm MDL}$); and $[\mathbf{D}]$ is the \emph{coherence measure}---a real-valued functional on candidate/record pairs that the Perfect Self-Containment (PSC) criterion uses to adjudicate which CA trajectories correspond to physical observations~\cite{SpivackGTEMobius}.  A \emph{$[\mathbf{D}]$-selected observable} is one assigned positive weight by $[\mathbf{D}]$.  The PSC axiom D2 (Presentation Invariance) states that $[\mathbf{D}]$ is invariant under any transformation that preserves the substrate's PSC structure---this is the axiom from which Lorentz equivariance is derived in~\S\ref{sec:lorentz}.  Throughout, \emph{machine-certification levels} follow the GTE convention: $[\mathrm{A}_{\rm Lean}]$ (Lean~4 proof, zero sorry), $[\mathrm{A/D}]$ (algebraically derivable, computationally confirmed), $[\mathrm{A}]$ (computationally confirmed, no Lean certification).

\subsection{Per-cell update algorithm}
