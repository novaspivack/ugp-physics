\subsection{Lagrangian and canonical stress-energy tensor}

The $\PHIMDL$ field is the $\ZS$-symmetric Klein--Gordon field with Lagrangian
density (in $+{-}{-}{-}$ Minkowski signature)
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} = \tfrac{1}{2}(\partial_\mu\Phi)(\partial^\mu\Phi) - V(\Phi),
  \qquad V(\Phi) = \frac{m_\varphi^2}{49}\bigl(1 - \cos 7\Phi\bigr),
\label{eq:lagrangian}
\end{equation}
with $m_\varphi = m_\tau = 1776.86$~MeV.
The potential has exactly seven degenerate minima at $\Phi_k = 2\pi k/7$ ($k = 0,\ldots,6$),
forming the $\ZS$ orbit.

The canonical stress-energy tensor follows from Noether's theorem~\cite{SpivackPhiMDLField}:
\begin{equation}
  \Tmunu = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial^\mu\Phi)}\partial_\nu\Phi
  - \eta_{\mu\nu}\mathcal{L}
  = \partial_\mu\Phi\,\partial_\nu\Phi - \eta_{\mu\nu}\mathcal{L}.
\label{eq:tmunu_def}
\end{equation}
Symmetry $\Tmunu = T_{\nu\mu}$ follows from the symmetry of $\partial_\mu\Phi\,\partial_\nu\Phi$
and the metric $\eta_{\mu\nu}$.
This is certified in Lean~4 (\lean{phimdl\_tmunu\_symmetric},
\lean{StressEnergyTensor.lean}, $\CatAL$, zero \texttt{sorry}).

\subsection{Component structure}

Expanding~\eqref{eq:tmunu_def} explicitly:
\begin{align}
  T_{00} &= \tfrac{1}{2}(\partial_t\Phi)^2 + \tfrac{1}{2}|\nabla\Phi|^2 + V(\Phi)
            &&\text{(energy density, always $\geq 0$)}
\label{eq:T00}\\
  T_{0i} &= \partial_t\Phi\,\partial_i\Phi
            &&\text{(momentum density)}
\label{eq:T0i}\\
  T_{ij} &= \partial_i\Phi\,\partial_j\Phi
            + \delta_{ij}\bigl[\tfrac{1}{2}(\partial_t\Phi)^2
            - \tfrac{1}{2}|\nabla\Phi|^2 + V(\Phi)\bigr]
  \;(-1)
            &&\text{(stress tensor)}
\label{eq:Tij}
\end{align}
where the overall $(-1)$ in~\eqref{eq:Tij} reflects the $(-,-,-)$ spatial
signature.
The trace is
\begin{equation}
  T \equiv \eta^{\mu\nu}\Tmunu = (\partial_t\Phi)^2 - |\nabla\Phi|^2 - 4V(\Phi).
\label{eq:trace}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{\texttt{phimdl\_kink\_gravitational\_source.py}.}

gravitational self-energy ratio $|U_{\rm grav}|/\Mkink \approx 3.4\times10^{-39}$.

\paragraph{\texttt{phimdl\_cosmological\_constant.py}.}

decoherence rate $\Gamma_{\rm OR} = \GN\Mkink^2 m_\varphi/\hbar^2 c$ and the

constants from CODATA/PDG.
