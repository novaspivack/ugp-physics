\begin{abstract}
We derive all four Wolfenstein parameters of the CKM quark mixing matrix from
the arithmetic of the Rule~110 cellular automaton orbit that the Standard Model
generation sequence is forced to satisfy~\cite{SpivackCompUniversality},
with zero free parameters.
The derivation proceeds in two stages.
First, the five quark-sector orbit indices $N_{\rm eff}$ are identified with
structural formulas in the GTE orbit constants $\Ngen = 3$, $\Nfam = 5$,
$\cH = 13$: the up quark at $\bu = \Ngen^2 = 9$; the down quark at
$\bd = \Nfam = 5$; the charm quark at $\bc = \Nfam^2(2\Nfam+1) = 275$;
the strange quark at $\bs = 2\Ngen(2\cH+\Nfam) = 186$; and the bottom quark
at $\bb = 2^{\cH}-1 = 8191$, a Mersenne prime at the Higgs staircase endpoint.
None of these values is a free parameter; all are consequences of the GTE
cascade structure, machine-certified in Lean~4 with zero \texttt{sorry}
for the arithmetic identities.

Second, the four Wolfenstein parameters follow.
The leading parameter
$\lam = \Ngen^2/(2^{\Ngen}\!\times\!\Nfam) = 9/40 = 0.22500$
agrees with the PDG value $0.22500\pm 0.00067$ to $\mathbf{0.000\%}$
($0.000\sigma$); it is machine-certified in Lean~4 (\lean{wolfenstein\_lambda\_formula},
zero \texttt{sorry}).
The subleading parameter $A = \sqrt{N_{\rm eff}(s)/N_{\rm eff}(c)} = \sqrt{186/275} = 0.8224$
matches PDG at $0.65\sigma$.
The remaining parameters $\Rbar$ and $\Ebar$ follow from a deep cross-sector
identity: the CKM unitarity triangle radius $\Rb = \Ngen/2^{\Ngen} = 3/8$ equals the
GUT-scale Weinberg mixing angle $\sinwsq(M_{\rm GUT}) = 3/8$
(machine-certified; \lean{ckm\_unitarity\_triangle\_radius\_eq\_gut\_weinberg}).
From $\Rb = 3/8$ and a Mersenne-to-algebraic ratio $\tan(\gamma) = \sqrt{\bb/\bs}/\Ngen$,
we obtain $\Rbar = 0.1545$ ($0.41\sigma$) and $\Ebar = 0.3417$ ($0.63\sigma$).
All nine CKM matrix elements agree with the PDG within $1\sigma$ at $O(\lam^4)$.
The CP angle $\gamma = 65.67^\circ$ lies $-0.023\sigma$ from the PDG central value.
The Mersenne primality of $\bb$ forces $\tan(\gamma)$ to be irrational,
providing an arithmetic origin for CP violation.
\end{abstract}

% ── 1. Introduction ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

The Cabibbo--Kobayashi--Maskawa (CKM) quark mixing matrix~\cite{Cabibbo1963,KobayashiMaskawa1973}
contains four independent parameters in its standard Wolfenstein
parametrization~\cite{Wolfenstein1983}: the mixing magnitude $\lam$, the
hierarchy parameter $A$, and the complex apex coordinates $\Rbar$, $\Ebar$
of the unitarity triangle.
In the Standard Model these four parameters are free inputs,
not derivable from gauge symmetry or any known principle.
Their measured values from the PDG~\cite{ParticleDataGroup2022} are
$\lam = 0.22500\pm 0.00067$, $A \approx 0.814\pm 0.013$,
$\Rbar = 0.159\pm 0.011$, $\Ebar = 0.348\pm 0.010$.

\medskip

The Universal Generative Principle (UGP)~\cite{Spivack2026_SM_UGP} derives the
Standard Model particle content from three axioms of locality, symmetry, and
compression (MDL minimality) applied to a number-theoretic sieve.
A key result in~\cite{SpivackCompUniversality} establishes that the SM generation
orbit $({\rm gen}_1 \to {\rm gen}_2 \to {\rm gen}_3)$ is forced to be a Rule~110
cellular automaton orbit, and that the MDL-minimal $\mathbb{Z}_7$ interaction
function $f_{\rm MDL}$ is uniquely determined (CUP-12, $\CatAL$, zero \texttt{sorry}).
The companion paper~\cite{SpivackWeinberg} derives the electroweak mixing angle
$\sinwsq = 3/13$ from the palindrome decomposition of the $f_{\rm MDL}$
neighborhoods, and the GUT-scale value $\sinwsq(M_{\rm GUT}) = 3/8 = \Ngen/2^{\Ngen}$
(machine-certified, zero \texttt{sorry}).

\medskip

The present paper extends this derivation to the CKM flavor sector.
The Generative Triple Evolution (GTE) cascade assigns an orbit index $N_{\rm eff}$
to each quark, determined by the GTE arithmetic constants
$\Ngen = 3$, $\Nfam = 5$, $\cH = 13$.
We identify five quark $N_{\rm eff}$ structural formulas (none fitted to quark masses)
and derive all four Wolfenstein parameters from them with zero free parameters.
