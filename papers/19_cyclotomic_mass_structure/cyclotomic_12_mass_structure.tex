\begin{abstract}
We present two structural mass relations that reduce the nine charged-fermion
masses to two empirical scale inputs and identify their cyclotomic-12 and
SU(5)/SO(10) origins.

The TT relation $\log(m_{u_g}/m_{\ell_g}) = (\pi/6)\cdot 2^g + \pi/8$
holds against PDG masses at $\leq 0.37\%$ max-fractional-error across six
$\beta$-free inter-generational identities (null density $6\times10^{-6}$).
The VV relation $\log(m_{d_g}) = \tfrac{13}{9}\log(m_{u_g}) - \tfrac{7}{6}\log(m_{\ell_g}) - \tfrac{5}{14}$
holds at $\leq 0.17\%$ across all three generations (null density $\leq 10^{-5}$).
Combined with the Koide closed form~\cite{Spivack2026_Koide},
all nine charged-fermion masses are determined from two scalar inputs.

The TT coefficient $\alpha = \pi/6$ is the half-opening angle of the $A_2$
Weyl chamber (Lean-certified: \lean{angle\_alpha1\_omega1\_eq\_pi\_div\_six},
zero sorry); the generation-doubling factor $2^g$ is proved via a binary
cascade (Lean-certified).
The VV coefficients $(13/9, -7/6, -5/14)$ satisfy exact SU(5)/SO(10)
group-theoretic identities (\lean{VV\_from\_GUT\_group\_theory}, zero sorry)
classified as structural facts \textbf{[T]}; the coefficient-value
interpretation is classified \textbf{[C]} (see \S\ref{sec:vv_honest}).
A pre-registered three-null-test discipline accompanies each derivation.
All Lean content is machine-checked at zero sorry in the \texttt{ugp-lean}
library~\cite{ugp-lean}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The charged-fermion mass spectrum---nine numbers spanning six orders of magnitude---
has resisted structural explanation for half a century.
Prior approaches include Georgi-Jarlskog~\cite{GeorgiJarlskog1979} and Fritzsch texture
zeros~\cite{Fritzsch1977} for quark masses, Froggatt-Nielsen flavon
mechanisms~\cite{Froggatt1979} for hierarchies, discrete flavour
symmetries~\cite{AltarelliFeruglio2010,Ishimori2010} for mixing,
and the Koide relation~\cite{Koide1983} as the most striking lepton-sector numerical
regularity---now machine-certified to yield $m_\tau$ from $(m_e,m_\mu)$
at 61~ppm~\cite{Spivack2026_Koide}.
Arithmetic and noncommutative geometric structures entering particle physics have also
been studied in the spectral-geometry program of Connes and Marcolli~\cite{ConnesMarcolli2008},
where the SM Lagrangian emerges from a spectral triple, and in $F_\zeta$-geometry
relating cyclotomic fields to motives~\cite{MarcolliManin2020}.
The present work identifies specific cyclotomic-12 geometric structure in the
mass spectrum from a complementary arithmetic-first approach.
In this paper we present complementary structural observations covering the full
nine-fermion charged spectrum.

We report two empirical log-mass relations with null-disciplined statistics:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{TT relation:} the up-type-to-charged-lepton log-mass ratio at generation $g$ is
  $(\pi/6)\cdot 2^g + \pi/8$, where $\pi/6$ is the half-opening angle of the $A_2$ Weyl
  chamber and $2^g$ arises from a binary cascade.
  \item \textbf{VV relation:} the down-type log-mass is a linear combination of up-type and
  lepton log-masses with rational coefficients $(13/9,-7/6,-5/14)$.  The
  \emph{formula} is a structural empirical regularity; the specific coefficient
  \emph{values} are post-hoc algebraic [C] (Section~\ref{sec:vv_honest}).
\end{itemize}

Together with the Koide closed form~\cite{Spivack2026_Koide}, these three relations compress
nine masses to two empirical inputs.  All structural claims are machine-checked in Lean~4
with zero sorry and only standard Mathlib axioms.

The central mathematical observation, independent of any physical framework, is that the
TT coefficient $\alpha=\pi/6$ and the Koide surd $(2+\sqrt{3})=4\cos^2(\pi/12)$ both
belong to the cyclotomic-12 atom family $\{\pi/12,\,\cos(\pi/12),\,\sqrt{3}\}$~\cite{Spivack2026_Koide},
while $\pi/6$ has a clean structural origin in the $A_2$ Weyl-chamber geometry of
$\mathrm{SU}(3)_{\rm flavor}$ and the generation factor $2^g$ is structurally
realised by a Froggatt-Nielsen UV completion with Lean-proved global-minimum VEVs.

For the VV coefficient values we perform three pre-registered null-discipline tests
(Sections~\ref{sec:jjj}--\ref{sec:three_strike}) demonstrating saturation, following
the methodology introduced in~\cite{Spivack2026_SM_UGP}.  The VV \emph{formula} is a
genuine structural regularity; the three specific coefficient \emph{values} are in the
saturating zone of every exhausted natural basis and are therefore classified as

\subsection{Relation 1 --- TT cyclotomic up-to-lepton identity}
\label{sec:tt}

Let $g\in\{1,2,3\}$ index the three charged-fermion generations and let $m_{u_g}$,
$m_{\ell_g}$ denote the up-type quark and charged-lepton masses at PDG central values.
Define the log-mass ratio

\begin{equation}
  R_g \;:=\; \log\!\paren{\frac{m_{u_g}}{m_{\ell_g}}}
  \qquad (g=1,\,2,\,3),
  \label{eq:TT_ratio}
\end{equation}

where $m_{u_1}=m_u$, $m_{u_2}=m_c$, $m_{u_3}=m_t$ and
$m_{\ell_1}=m_e$, $m_{\ell_2}=m_\mu$, $m_{\ell_3}=m_\tau$ at PDG~2022 central

$Z_6\times Z_{16}$-invariant Cartan-torus potential (Section~\ref{sec:cartan}).

\section{Derivation of the $A_2$ Weyl-Chamber Angle}
\label{app:a2}

We give an explicit two-dimensional construction of eq.~\eqref{eq:A2_angle}.

The $A_2$ root system in the Cartan plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ has simple roots
$\alpha_1=(1,0)$ and $\alpha_2=(-1/2,\sqrt{3}/2)$ (at $120^\circ$).
The fundamental weight $\omega_1$ dual to $\alpha_1$ satisfies
$\langle\omega_1,\alpha_1^\vee\rangle=1$ and $\langle\omega_1,\alpha_2^\vee\rangle=0$,
where $\alpha^\vee = 2\alpha/\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle$.
In Cartesian coordinates: $\omega_1 = (1/2,\,\sqrt{3}/6)$
(one can verify $\langle\omega_1,\alpha_1\rangle/|\alpha_1|^2=1/2$
and $\langle\omega_1,\alpha_2\rangle/|\alpha_2|^2=0$).

The angle between $\alpha_1=(1,0)$ and $\omega_1=(1/2,\sqrt{3}/6)$ is
\[
  \theta = \arctan\!\paren{\frac{\sqrt{3}/6}{1/2}} = \arctan\!\paren{\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}} = \frac{\pi}{6}.
\]

The fundamental Weyl chamber is bounded by the half-planes
$\langle v, \alpha_i\rangle \geq 0$ for $i=1,2$, which in polar coordinates
corresponds to angles $\theta\in[0,\pi/3]$.  The interior bisector is at
$\theta = \pi/6$, i.e., $\omega_1$ lies on the chamber bisector.

This is the content of \lean{angle\_alpha1\_omega1\_eq\_pi\_div\_six}
and \lean{omega1\_in\_weyl\_chamber\_interior}
(both in \lean{UgpLean.MassRelations.SU3FlavorCartan}).
