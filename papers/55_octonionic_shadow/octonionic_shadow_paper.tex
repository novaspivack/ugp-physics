\begin{abstract}

The UGP/GTE arithmetic programme derives Standard Model structure from
GF(7) and the group $F_{21} = \mathbb{Z}_7\!\rtimes\!\mathbb{Z}_3$, with
major claims machine-certified in Lean~4.
The division-algebra programme (Dixon; Furey; Baez--Huerta) independently
derives one-generation Standard Model structure from the octonions.
This paper proves the two programmes are facets of a single finite structure:
the quadratic-residue set $\mathrm{QR}(7) = \{1,2,4\} \subset \mathbb{F}_7^*$
is simultaneously the arithmetic anchor of GTE and the Fano-plane orientation
that defines the octonion algebra.

Starting from $D = \{1,2,4\}$ as the sole input, we establish a six-link
machine-verified derivation chain: \emph{(i)}~the oriented translates of $D$
are the octonion multiplication table, uniquely up to relabeling, with the
sign encoding $\mathrm{sign}(e_x e_y) = [y-x\in D]$ read directly from the
difference set; \emph{(ii)}~$N_c = 3$ from the pencil count of the $(7,3,1)$
design via $F_{21}\hookrightarrow G_2$ and $\mathrm{Stab}_{G_2}(\mathrm{apex})
= \mathrm{SU}(3)$; \emph{(iii)}~the exceptional isomorphism
$\mathrm{PSL}(2,7)\cong\mathrm{GL}(3,2)$, order~168, certified by
Todd--Coxeter coset enumeration; \emph{(iv)}~the derivation algebra
$\mathrm{der}(\mathbb{O}) = \mathfrak{g}_2$ (dimension~14 exact) and
$\mathrm{Stab}_{G_2}(\mathrm{apex}) = \mathfrak{su}(3)$ (dimension~8 exact),
with bracket closure and Killing-form certificate computed from first
principles~(\CatAL, 39 Lean theorems); \emph{(v)}~UGP's flavor package
$(\mu_3, U, V_4, A_4)$ is equivariantly isomorphic to the Spin(8) triality
package $(\rho, \sigma, Z(\mathrm{Spin}(8)), Z\!\rtimes\!\rho)$, pinning
$\mathrm{gen}_1\leftrightarrow V$ via Eisenstein norm uniqueness
$b_{\mathrm{gen}_1} = 73 = \mathcal{N}(9+\omega)$~(\CatAL); and
\emph{(vi)}~the charged-lepton Koide mass ladder is a function on the
triality orbit with torsor-invariant angle $|\delta\bmod 2\pi/3|
= 0.222229631 = 2/9 + 7.4\times10^{-6}$ (7~ppm from PDG).

Two structurally independent derivation paths --- finite arithmetic (GTE) and
continuous division algebra --- converge on identical outputs $(N_c, \text{charge
spectrum}, N_{\mathrm{gen}}=3)$; this convergence constitutes non-trivial
structural evidence that these quantities are genuine consequences of
the GF(7) arithmetic rather than numerical accidents.
The neutrino sector is completed by the GTE seesaw: the right-handed seed ordering
$b_{R,1}=5<b_{R,2}=11<b_{R,3}=19$ pins the mass eigenstates $\nu_k$ via
$m_{\nu,k}\propto b_{R,k}^{29/9}$ and predicts normal ordering,
with $\Delta m^2_{21}$ and $\Delta m^2_{31}$ within $1\%$ of NuFIT~6.0~IC24~NH.
All major claims are certified at level \CatAL{} (zero \texttt{sorry} in Lean~4)
or \CatA{} (computation-verified by assertion-checked Python scripts).
\S\ref{sec:honest} lists what is not established.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
%=============================================================================

Two research programmes derive Standard Model structure from economical
inputs, and have until now developed without systematic contact.

\paragraph{The GTE/UGP arithmetic programme.}
Starting from the finite field $\mathbb{F}_7$ and its quadratic-residue set
$\mathrm{QR}(7) = \{1,2,4\}$, the GTE framework \cite{SpivackGTECompleteFramework}
constructs the gauge skeleton $F_{21} = \mathbb{Z}_7\!\rtimes\!\mathbb{Z}_3$,
derives $N_c=3$, the SM charge spectrum, and $N_{\mathrm{gen}}=3$ from
consistency constraints, and certifies the algebraic layer in Lean~4
\cite{SpivackUGPFormalization,ugp-lean}.
The programme derives \emph{what} the numbers are from first principles
\cite{Spivack2025UGP}, but the connection from finite arithmetic to the continuous
symmetry groups of particle physics has required individual geometric
justifications at each step.

\paragraph{The division-algebra programme.}
Starting from the four normed division algebras $\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C},
\mathbb{H}, \mathbb{O}$ \cite{Hurwitz1898}, Dixon \cite{Dixon1994}, Furey
\cite{Furey2015,Furey2016thesis,Furey2018}, Baez and Huerta
\cite{BaezHuerta2010,Baez2002}, Manogue and Dray \cite{ManogueDray1999},
and Furey and Hughes \cite{FureyHughes2025} derive one-generation Standard
Model structure: $\mathrm{SU}(3) = \mathrm{Stab}_{G_2}(e_7)$,
charges from Clifford-algebra number operators, and the weak sector from
the quaternionic factor.
The programme reveals \emph{how} the symmetry groups fit geometrically, but
does not explain \emph{why} the octonions: Hurwitz's theorem establishes
that $\mathbb{O}$ is unique among normed division algebras of its dimension,
but does not supply a discrete origin for the octonion structure
\cite{Baez2002,Hurwitz1898}.

\paragraph{The bridge.}
This paper proves the two programmes share a common finite root: the
difference set $D = \mathrm{QR}(7) = \{1,2,4\}\subset\mathbb{F}_7^*$
is simultaneously (a) the arithmetic anchor of the GTE framework, and
(b) the Fano-plane orientation that uniquely determines the octonion
multiplication table.
The GTE gauge skeleton $F_{21}\hookrightarrow G_2 = \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O})$
embeds as a subgroup of octonion automorphisms (Theorems D, E); the division-algebra
structure appears \emph{as a consequence of the GF(7) arithmetic}, not as an
independent starting point.

\paragraph{Why this matters: six points.}
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
  \item \textit{The division-algebra programme's foundational gap.}
    Hurwitz's theorem establishes $\mathbb{O}$ as the unique
    8-dimensional normed division algebra, but does not explain why nature
    chose it. The programme finds the SM inside the octonions but lacks a discrete
    foundation for why these algebras exist.
  \item \textit{GTE's gap (before this paper).}
    GTE derives the SM numerics from $\mathbb{F}_7$, but the connection from
    finite arithmetic to continuous Lie groups (SU(3), SU(2), $G_2$)
    has required individual justifications at each stage.
  \item \textit{Mutual resolution.}
    The present work shows: \emph{why octonions?} --- Because
    $\mathrm{QR}(7)\subset\mathbb{F}_7^*$ forces the Fano geometry, and
    only the 7-dimensional case yields a normed division algebra when oriented
    combinatorially. \emph{Why $\mathbb{F}_7$?} --- Because only $\mathbb{F}_7$'s
    quadratic residues form a $(7,3,1)$-design whose oriented translates give
    a normed division algebra. The two structures are two projections of one object.
  \item \textit{Independent convergence as structural evidence.}
    When two completely independent paths --- finite arithmetic (GTE) and
    continuous algebra (division algebras) --- converge on identical outputs
    ($N_c=3$, the SM charge spectrum, three generation slots from triality),
    that convergence is hard to dismiss as coincidence. This constitutes
    the strongest kind of structural evidence that these quantities are necessary
    rather than contingent.
  \item \textit{Tower termination unified --- an external anchor for ``why~7?''}
    The GTE programme's internal justifications for $\mathbb{F}_7$
    (PSC constraints, FGCI identity, orbit structure) are self-consistency
    conditions: they establish that $7$ satisfies GTE's own architectural
    requirements from within the framework.
    The octonionic bridge supplies a justification external to that architecture.
    Hurwitz's theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:F4}) says the normed division algebra
    tower $\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C},\mathbb{H},\mathbb{O}$ terminates at
    dimension~8: sedenion zero-divisors destroy composition and no
    $\mathbb{O}$-successor exists.
    The bridge translates this into the GTE language: $\mathrm{GF}(7)$ is the
    last rung because the tower itself ends there.
    An answer to ``why~7?'' that makes no reference to GTE's internal structure:
    7 is the unique prime $p$ whose quadratic residues orient a normed division
    algebra, and the octonions are the terminal object of the only such tower
    mathematics permits.
    The base-rate analysis (\S\ref{sec:honest}) sharpens this: of the 13 named
    joints in the derivation chain
    $\mathrm{QR}(7)\!\to\!\mathrm{Fano}\!\to\!\mathbb{O}\!\to\!G_2\!\to\!\mathrm{SU}(3)$,
    eleven are mathematical theorems with no free parameter.
    The framework's foundation transforms from a self-consistent construction
    to a forced structure.
  \item \textit{Symmetry groups as automorphisms of an arithmetic object.}
    The standard SM formulation declares $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times
    \mathrm{U}(1)$ as a choice. Here, $\mathrm{SU}(3) = \mathrm{Stab}_{G_2}(\mathrm{apex})$
    where $G_2 = \mathrm{Aut}(\mathrm{QR}(7)\text{-Fano-octonions})$, and
    $\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ falls out of the quaternionic subalgebra
    (Theorems F1--F5). The gauge symmetry group is the automorphism group of an
    arithmetic object that had to exist.
\end{enumerate}

\section{GF(7) and the Fano Plane}

\begin{theorem}[Design; \CatAL]
\label{thm:A}
Let $D = \{1,2,4\}\subset\mathbb{Z}/7$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),noitemsep]
  \item $D$ is the set of nonzero quadratic residues modulo~7
        (\emph{i.e.}\ the squares in $(\mathbb{Z}/7)^*$).
  \item $D$ is a perfect $(7,3,1)$ planar difference set: every nonzero
        residue occurs as a difference of elements of $D$ in exactly one way.
  \item The seven translates $\{D+t : t\in\mathbb{Z}/7\}$ are the seven
        lines of a Fano plane on $\mathbb{Z}/7$.
  \item Exactly $|D| = 3$ lines pass through every point (the \emph{pencil}).
  \item The multiplier group of $D$ is $D$ itself: multiplication by $2$
        (order~3) preserves the design (Hall's multiplier theorem).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\lean{fanoLines\_are\_D\_translates}, \lean{pencil\_card}), all \texttt{decide}-class
(\CatAL).
The UGP corpus labels its lines as translates of $\{0,1,3\} = D-1$; the two

\begin{theorem}[Octonions from QR(7); \CatA]
\label{thm:B}
Orienting each line $(t{+}1, t{+}2, t{+}4)$ cyclically and extending by
anticommutativity, $e_i^2 = -1$, defines an 8-dimensional real algebra that
is alternative, Moufang, and norm-composing --- the octonions.
Among the $30 \cdot 2^7 = 3840$ oriented Fano tables, exactly $480$ are
composition algebras; the signed-permutation group (order $7!\cdot 2^7$)
acts transitively on them; the stabilizer (frame group) has order
$1344 = 2^3\cdot 168$ with sign-only kernel $2^3$ and quotient
$\mathrm{Aut}(\mathrm{Fano})$.
The octonion structure attached to $\mathrm{GF}(7)$ is therefore unique up
to relabeling.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Orientation $=$ QR; \CatAL]
\label{thm:Bprime}
In the closed-form index-level product,
$\mathrm{sign}(e_x e_y) = +1$ if and only if $y - x \in \mathrm{QR}(7)$.
The difference set is not merely the seed for the plane; it is the orientation
of the algebra.
\end{theorem}
