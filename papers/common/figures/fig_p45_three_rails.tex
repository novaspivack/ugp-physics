  winding readout $(w_x,w_y,w_z)$ on the three tapes---not a cell in a
  three-dimensional lattice. A \emph{particle} is a specific non-vacuum
  excitation of that structure (e.g.\ uniform triple $(w,w,w)$ for SM states;
