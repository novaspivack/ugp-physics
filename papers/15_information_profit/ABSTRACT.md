# Abstract — The Information Profit Principle

**Title:** The Information Profit Principle: Derivation for Reflexive Systems and Empirical Validation Across Self-Maintaining Systems

**Author:** Nova Spivack

**Year:** 2026

---

A reflexive self-maintaining system — one that must process information about its own state to remain coherent — must generate information structure faster than it dissipates it. This paper makes that intuition precise within the Mathematical Foundations of Reflexive Reality (MFRR) framework, deriving a sharp dimensionless threshold called the Information Profit Threshold (IPT) from two structural inputs of the MFRR model: the Reflexive Landauer Bound and the Perfect Self-Containment (PSC) requirement.

The derivation yields:

    IPT = 1 + Lambda/2,    Lambda = ln(phi) / ln(2*pi)  ≈  0.2618

where phi = (1 + sqrt(5)) / 2 is the golden ratio, giving IPT ≈ 1.1309. A system whose generative-to-drain ratio rho = G/D satisfies rho > IPT has an energy budget compatible with sustaining coherent self-referential processing; one with rho ≤ IPT cannot.

**Scope and validation.** Three independent computational experiments are presented, all conducted within the ugp-physics MFRR simulation framework:

1. TE1.H — a 2D toy field simulator testing three qualitatively distinct parameter regimes (unprofitable rho ≈ 0.80, profitable rho ≈ 1.40, high-noise rho < 1.00), confirming coherence growth above and decay below the threshold.

2. TE2.1 — a multi-run evolutionary neural-agent experiment (N = 124 genesis runs) whose surviving agent populations settle at profit ratios consistent with the supercritical regime (15/124 runs exceed IPT; supercritical mean rho = 1.234).

3. E4 — a 50-trial parametric sweep verifying that the Reflexive Landauer energy model underlying the derivation is internally consistent across randomized configurations, with all modeled PT costs exceeding the bound by strictly positive margins (min margin 0.018).

**Claims and scope.** All three validations are simulation-only; they are internal consistency tests of the MFRR model, not independent physical experiments. The IPT is a consequence of the MFRR modeling inputs (PSC requirement, Reflexive Landauer Bound), not of thermodynamics alone. No biological or economic experimental data were collected.

**Interpretive connections to biological metabolism and economic sustainability are explicitly conjectural.** They are discussed as analogies requiring independent experimental test and are not supported by data in this paper.

The derivation identifies a clear falsification criterion at both the theoretical level (a proof that PSC fixed-point iteration does not require a golden-ratio convergence surplus would revise the threshold) and the computational level (a simulation satisfying all MFRR assumptions but exhibiting coherence growth with rho ≤ IPT would constitute disconfirming evidence). Open quantitative questions include the derivation of the coherence coupling constant lambda_Psi from first principles, finite-time corrections to IPT, multi-agent network generalizations, and the behavior of the threshold under stochastic fluctuations.
