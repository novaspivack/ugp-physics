Written as an original synthesis essay connecting the GTE framework (P48) with the
NEMS phenomenology and consciousness programme (Papers 55–92 of the Reflexive Reality
suite). The argument was developed from first principles by reading:

- P48 (`papers/48_gte_complete_theory/gte_complete_theory.tex`)

machine-verified Lean proofs in `reflexive-closure-lean`, `sentience-lean`,
`phenomenology-lean`, `nems-lean`, and `transputation-lean`.
