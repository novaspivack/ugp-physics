\begin{abstract}
We examine black hole information unitarity within the Mathematical Foundations
of Reflexive Reality (MFRR) framework~\cite{SpivackNEMSMFRRBridge}.
The MFRR Transputation operator $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ provides a physical mechanism
for unitary information recovery consistent with the Perfect Self-Containment
condition. Since every CPTP map admits a Stinespring dilation (a mathematical
theorem independent of gravitational modelling), Hawking evaporation is
unitarily equivalent to a reversible evolution on a system-plus-environment space.

We construct the evaporation channel $\Phi_{\Delta t} = e^{\Delta t\mathcal{L}}$
as a GKSL master equation for a JT-like system of $n=3$ bosonic modes
($d_{\mathcal{H}} = 8$) at $T_H \approx 0.004$.
Stinespring dilation achieves fidelity $F \geq 1 - 10^{-8}$; detailed balance
holds to 0.00\%; the entropy curve reaches $97.2\%$ of the ideal thermal value.
In two complementary closed unitary models --- a pure initial BH state and a
purified thermal initial state with reference system $R$ --- the full Page curve
with turnover is reproduced and $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ recovery achieves $F = 1.000000$.

Open fronts are stated explicitly: extension to $3+1$ quantum gravity, the firewall
tension, and a first-principles derivation of $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The black hole information paradox \cite{Hawking1974,Hawking1976}
stands as one of the deepest unresolved tensions in theoretical physics.
Hawking's semiclassical calculation \cite{Hawking1975} predicts that an
evaporating black hole emits thermal radiation, apparently destroying
all correlations with the infalling matter. If correct, this would
violate unitarity---the cornerstone of quantum mechanics. Page argued
\cite{Page1993} that if information is ultimately preserved, the
entanglement entropy of Hawking radiation must rise and then fall along
what is now called the Page curve.

Modern approaches to this tension include:
the island formula and quantum extremal surfaces
\cite{Penington2020,AlmheiriEngelhartsMarolfMaxfield2019}
(which reproduce the Page curve in holographic settings);
the ER=EPR proposal \cite{MaldacenaSusskind2013}, connecting horizon
entanglement to non-traversable wormholes; and the firewall paradox
\cite{AlmheiriMarolfPolchinskiSully2013}, sharpening the tension
between unitarity and the equivalence principle. Despite significant
progress, a fully non-perturbative, background-independent resolution
remains elusive.

In this paper we examine the information paradox from the perspective
of the Mathematical Foundations of Reflexive Reality (MFRR)
\cite{SpivackNEMSMFRRBridge,SpivackPhysicsPortal}. The MFRR framework
introduces a Perfect Self-Containment (PSC) condition and an
internal adjudication operator $\mathrm{PT}$ governing the coherence
of self-referential structures. The canonical inverse $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$
provides a physical mechanism by which information encoded in a PSC
universe is not merely mathematically recoverable but is structurally
required to be recovered---we call this property \emph{reflexive
unitarity}.

Our central observation is that reflexive unitarity, combined with the
Stinespring dilation theorem \cite{Stinespring1955}, yields a concrete
programme: model Hawking evaporation as a GKSL (Gorini-Kossakowski-
Sudarshan-Lindblad) master equation \cite{Gorini1976,Lindblad1976},
note that the resulting channel is CPTP and therefore admits a
Stinespring dilation by theorem, and then use $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ to
interpret which unitary extension is physically realised. We implement
this programme explicitly in a JT-like \cite{JackiwTeitelboim1985,Teitelboim1983}
PSC toy universe and report the computational results of experiment TE2.4
\cite{SpivackBHUnitarity}.

We emphasise from the outset what this work does and does not claim.
The Stinespring theorem guarantees a unitary dilation for \emph{any}
CPTP map; this is not new. The novelties are: (i) the MFRR
interpretation of $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ as the physical recovery operator,
and (ii) the explicit construction and numerical verification of the
Stinespring unitary for the JT-like model. Extension to realistic
$3{+}1$ quantum gravity is an open problem.

The paper is organised as follows. Section~\ref{sec:mfrr} introduces
the PSC framework and the $\mathrm{PT}/\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ mechanism.
Section~\ref{sec:gksl} reviews GKSL dynamics and Stinespring dilation
and describes the JT-like model. Section~\ref{sec:te24} presents the
TE2.4 results. Section~\ref{sec:comparison} situates the MFRR approach
relative to existing approaches. Section~\ref{sec:scope} discusses scope
limitations and open fronts. Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes.
Appendices collect model parameters, construction details, and
reproducibility information.

\section{The PSC Framework and Reflexive Unitarity}
\label{sec:mfrr}

\subsection{The Perfect Self-Containment Condition}

The MFRR framework \cite{SpivackNEMSMFRRBridge} posits that a
physically realised universe must satisfy a Perfect Self-Containment
(PSC) condition: the universe's global state $\Omega$ must remain
coherent under its own adjudication at every moment. Formally, the PSC
condition requires that the informational structure of $\Omega$ be
self-sustaining---no external selector is permitted; the universe must
select its own internal laws \cite{SpivackNEMSMFRRBridge}.

Within this framework, Transputation ($\mathrm{PT}$) is an internal adjudication
operator that evaluates whether a proposed state transition is
self-consistent. It acts as a kind of internal filter: only transitions
that preserve PSC are realised. The operator $\mathrm{PT}$ is not a
Hamiltonian or a density matrix; it is a higher-level structural
constraint that acts on the space of possible universe histories.

\subsection{The Canonical Reverse \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$}{PT-inverse}}

A key feature of the MFRR construction is that $\mathrm{PT}$ admits a
canonical inverse $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$. This inverse is not merely a
formal algebraic object; it represents the physical process by which a
PSC universe \emph{undoes} the apparent information loss introduced by
open-system dynamics. Concretely, $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ implements the
recovery channel that restores the full quantum information of the
infalling matter from the Hawking radiation, \emph{given access to the
complete environment}.

The critical interpretive point is this: $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ does not
violate the second law or the semiclassical approximation. Rather, it
operates at the level of the full PSC universe (system plus
environment), where unitary evolution is restored by the Stinespring
construction. The apparent non-unitarity of the Hawking channel is, in
this picture, a consequence of tracing out the environment; the
underlying evolution on $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}} \otimes
\mathcal{H}_E$ remains unitary.

\subsection{Reflexive Unitarity}

We define \emph{reflexive unitarity} as the property that a PSC
universe, evolving under GKSL dynamics on its system Hilbert space,
admits a unitary dilation on the enlarged space
$\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}} \otimes \mathcal{H}_E$ such that the
$\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ operator coincides with the environment-tracing
adjoint of this dilation. This is not an additional postulate: it
follows from the Stinespring theorem (the dilation exists for any
CPTP channel) together with the PSC requirement (which selects a
specific physical interpretation for the environment degrees of
freedom).

\begin{definition}[Reflexive Unitarity]
A quantum channel $\Phi: \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}}) \to
\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}})$ satisfies \emph{reflexive
unitarity} with respect to a PSC adjudication pair
$(\mathrm{PT},\mathrm{PT}^{-1})$ if there exists a Hilbert space
$\mathcal{H}_E$ and a unitary $U \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}}
\otimes \mathcal{H}_E)$ such that
\[
  \Phi(\rho) = \mathrm{Tr}_E\!\left[U\!\left(\rho \otimes
  |0\rangle\langle 0|_E\right)U^\dagger\right]
\]
and $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ is identified with the adjoint recovery map
$\rho_E \mapsto \mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{sys}}[U^\dagger(\cdot)U]$
restricted to the environment.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
The existence of $U$ is guaranteed by the Stinespring theorem for any
CPTP channel $\Phi$ \cite{Stinespring1955,Nielsen2000}. The content of
reflexive unitarity is not the existence of $U$ but the physical
identification of $U$ with the $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ recovery process
within the PSC framework. This identification is \emph{interpretive
rather than structural}: the Stinespring theorem guarantees the
existence of some $U$ for any CPTP map; PSC provides the physical
rationale for which $U$ is physically realized, without imposing
additional mathematical constraints on $U$ beyond those of the
Stinespring construction. The novel content is the identification, not
the existence of the dilation.
\end{remark}

\section{GKSL Dynamics and Stinespring Dilation}
\label{sec:gksl}

\subsection{The Stinespring Dilation Theorem}

We recall the relevant mathematical fact.

\begin{theorem}[Stinespring 1955 \cite{Stinespring1955}]
Let $\Phi: \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H}) \to \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$ be a
completely positive trace-preserving map. Then there exists a Hilbert
space $\mathcal{H}_E$ and an isometry $V: \mathcal{H} \to \mathcal{H}
\otimes \mathcal{H}_E$ such that
\[
  \Phi(\rho) = \mathrm{Tr}_E\!\left[V\rho V^\dagger\right].
\]
The isometry $V$ extends to a unitary $U$ on $\mathcal{H}\otimes
\mathcal{H}_E$ by choosing any completion of $V$ to a square unitary,
giving $\Phi(\rho) = \mathrm{Tr}_E[U(\rho\otimes|0\rangle\langle
0|_E)U^\dagger]$.
\end{theorem}

A consequence familiar from quantum information theory
\cite{Nielsen2000} is the Kraus representation: for any CPTP map
there exist Kraus operators $\{K_k\}$ with $\sum_k K_k^\dagger K_k = I$
such that $\Phi(\rho) = \sum_k K_k \rho K_k^\dagger$. The isometry
is then $V|\psi\rangle = \sum_k K_k|\psi\rangle \otimes |k\rangle_E$,
and $\dim\mathcal{H}_E$ equals the number of Kraus operators.

\subsection{GKSL Master Equation}

The GKSL (Lindblad) master equation \cite{Gorini1976,Lindblad1976}
describes the most general Markovian, completely positive, trace-preserving
evolution of an open quantum system:
\begin{align}
  \frac{d\rho}{dt} &= \mathcal{L}[\rho], \nonumber \\
  \mathcal{L}[\rho] &:= -i[H,\rho] + \sum_k \gamma_k\!\left(
    L_k \rho L_k^\dagger \right. \nonumber \\
  &\qquad \left. - \tfrac{1}{2} L_k^\dagger L_k \rho
    - \tfrac{1}{2} \rho L_k^\dagger L_k
  \right),
  \label{eq:gksl}
\end{align}
where $H$ is the system Hamiltonian, $L_k$ are Lindblad jump operators,
and $\gamma_k \geq 0$ are rates. The propagator $\Phi_t = e^{t\mathcal{L}}$
is a one-parameter semigroup of CPTP maps for all $t \geq 0$.

Because $\Phi_t$ is CPTP, the Stinespring theorem guarantees a unitary
dilation for each $t$. For a small time step $\Delta t$, the Kraus
operators take the explicit form
\begin{align}
  K_0 &= I - \left(iH + \tfrac{1}{2}\sum_k \gamma_k L_k^\dagger L_k\right)\Delta t,
  \label{eq:kraus0}\\
  K_k &= \sqrt{\gamma_k \Delta t}\; L_k, \quad k = 1,\ldots,m.
  \label{eq:krausl}
\end{align}
The environment dimension for this step is $\dim\mathcal{H}_E = 1 + m$,
where $m$ is the number of distinct Lindblad operators.

\begin{remark}[Approximation order]
The Kraus operators in \eqref{eq:kraus0}--\eqref{eq:krausl} are the
first-order ($O(\Delta t)$) approximation of the exact GKSL propagator
$\Phi_{\Delta t} = e^{\Delta t \mathcal{L}}$. The truncated channel
differs from the exact channel by $O(\Delta t^2) \approx 10^{-4}$ (for
$\Delta t = 0.01$). The Stinespring dilation constructed in TE2.4 is exact
for this truncated channel; it agrees with the dilation of the full GKSL
channel to $O(\Delta t^2)$ precision.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The JT-like PSC Model}
\label{sec:model}

\looseness=-1
Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT) gravity \cite{JackiwTeitelboim1985,Teitelboim1983}
is a $1{+}1$-dimensional dilaton gravity model that captures key features
of near-extremal black holes and has served as a theoretical laboratory
for the information paradox \cite{Penington2020}. We use a JT-like PSC
toy universe that retains the qualitative structure---a system Hilbert
space $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}}$ representing degrees of freedom near
the black hole horizon, a Hawking temperature $T_H$, and thermal emission
and absorption Lindblad operators---while operating in a tractable
finite-dimensional setting.

The system consists of $n = 3$ bosonic modes, each truncated to
$d = 2$ Fock levels (vacuum $|0\rangle$ and single-excitation $|1\rangle$),
giving total Hilbert space dimension $d_{\mathcal{H}} = d^n = 8$.
The Hamiltonian is a sum of harmonic oscillators at frequencies
$\omega_j = (j + \tfrac{1}{2})\pi T_H$, $j = 0,1,2$, with $T_H =
0.003979$ in natural units where the fundamental frequency is $1$ (so
that mode frequencies are $\omega_j = (j+\tfrac{1}{2})\pi T_H$). The
lowest mode frequency is $\omega_0 = \tfrac{1}{2}\pi T_H \approx 0.00625$,
giving $T_H/\omega_0 \approx 0.64$. The thermal occupation of the lowest
mode is $\bar{n}_0 = 1/(e^{\omega_0/T_H}-1) \approx 0.26$, placing the
system in a moderate-temperature regime.

For each mode $j$, two Lindblad operators model the Hawking process:
\begin{align}
  L^{(j)}_{\mathrm{emit}} &= \sqrt{\gamma_{\mathrm{emit},j}}\; a_j,
  \label{eq:lemit}\\
  L^{(j)}_{\mathrm{abs}} &= \sqrt{\gamma_{\mathrm{abs},j}}\; a_j^\dagger,
  \label{eq:labs}
\end{align}
where $a_j$, $a_j^\dagger$ are the lowering and raising operators for
mode $j$, and the rates satisfy the detailed balance condition
$\gamma_{\mathrm{emit},j}/\gamma_{\mathrm{abs},j} = e^{-\omega_j/T_H}$,
with coupling strength $\gamma_0 = 0.01$. The total number of Lindblad
operators is $m = 2n = 6$, giving
\begin{equation}
  \dim\mathcal{H}_E = 1 + m = 7.
  \label{eq:dimE}
\end{equation}

The detailed balance condition ensures the GKSL dynamics drives the
system toward the Gibbs thermal state $\rho_{\mathrm{th}} \propto
e^{-H/T_H}$, analogous to a black hole relaxing to a thermal
Hawking state.

\section{TE2.4 Computational Results}
\label{sec:te24}

Experiment TE2.4 \cite{SpivackBHUnitarity} implements the programme described
above. All code is archived in the ugp-physics repository
\cite{ugp-physics-repo}; the canonical result file is
\texttt{results/phase2\_3\_final/final\_results.json};
the SHA-256 hash is listed in Appendix~\ref{app:repro}.

\subsection{Validation of CPTP Property}

The GKSL channel $\Phi_{\Delta t}$ with $\Delta t = 0.01$ was verified
to be CPTP by checking that, for all test states (vacuum, thermal, Fock
$|1,0,0\rangle$), the evolved state satisfies: (i) trace preservation
to error $< 10^{-10}$; (ii) all eigenvalues $\geq -10^{-10}$ (positive
semidefiniteness); and (iii) Hermiticity error $< 10^{-10}$. Detailed
balance was verified to $0.00\%$ relative error.

\subsection{Stinespring Construction and Fidelity}

The Stinespring dilation was constructed explicitly as follows. For
time step $\Delta t = 0.01$, the Kraus operators $K_0$ and $K_1,\ldots,K_6$
were computed using equations \eqref{eq:kraus0}--\eqref{eq:krausl}.
The isometry $V: \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}} \to \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}}
\otimes \mathcal{H}_E$ was assembled as the block-column matrix
\begin{equation}
  V = \begin{pmatrix} K_0 \\ K_1 \\ \vdots \\ K_6 \end{pmatrix},
  \label{eq:isometry}
\end{equation}
a $56 \times 8$ complex matrix ($8 \times 7$ block rows of width 8).
The unitary dilation $U$ was obtained by completing $V$ to a square
unitary on $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}} \otimes \mathcal{H}_E$ via
QR-based Gram-Schmidt extension.

To verify the dilation, the evolved state was computed via both the GKSL
propagator and the Stinespring construction (partial trace of
$U(\rho\otimes|0\rangle\langle 0|_E)U^\dagger$), and the fidelity
\begin{equation}
  F(\rho,\sigma) = \left(\mathrm{Tr}\sqrt{\sqrt{\rho}\,\sigma\sqrt{\rho}}\right)^2
  \label{eq:fidelity}
\end{equation}
was computed between the two results for three representative states:
the vacuum state $|0,0,0\rangle\langle 0,0,0|$, the Gibbs thermal state
at $T_H$, and the Fock state $|1,0,0\rangle\langle 1,0,0|$.

The results, as recorded in \texttt{final\_results.json}, are:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Test State & $F$ & Status \\
\midrule
Vacuum $|0,0,0\rangle$ & $\approx 1.0$ & PASS \\
Fock $|1,0,0\rangle$   & $1.0$ & PASS \\
Thermal $\rho_{\mathrm{th}}$ & $1.0$ & PASS \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

All three fidelities meet and exceed the threshold $F \geq 1 - 10^{-8}$
(the reported numerical values are $\geq 1.0$ to double-precision floating
point). The slight super-unity value for the vacuum state
($F = 1.0000000000000002$) is a rounding artefact of floating-point
arithmetic and lies within double-precision machine epsilon
($\epsilon_{\mathrm{mach}} \approx 2.2\times 10^{-16}$).

\begin{remark}
The fidelity $F \geq 1-10^{-8}$ is the verification threshold used in
the experiment; the actual numerical results saturate this threshold at
the level of double-precision arithmetic. This reflects the exactness
of the Kraus construction at the algebraic level, not merely an
approximate numerical agreement.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Thermalization Entropy Curve}

Starting from the vacuum state, the GKSL dynamics was run for $t \in
[0,200]$ with time step $\delta t = 0.05$. The steady-state fidelity
with the Gibbs thermal state $\rho_{\mathrm{th}}$ is
\begin{equation}
  F_{\mathrm{th}} = 0.9999192951,
  \label{eq:Fth}
\end{equation}
confirming near-perfect thermalization. The steady-state purity is
$0.7142$ and the von Neumann entropy is $0.4945$ nats.

The entanglement entropy was tracked along the evolution. Key values:
\begin{align*}
  S(0) &= 0 \quad (\text{vacuum}), \\
  S_{\mathrm{peak}} &= 0.4462 \text{ nats} \quad \text{at } t = 200, \\
  S_{\mathrm{thermal}} &= 0.4592 \text{ nats}, \\
  S_{\mathrm{peak}}/S_{\mathrm{thermal}} &= 0.9717.
\end{align*}
The entropy rises monotonically from zero toward the thermal value,
reaching $97.2\%$ of $S_{\mathrm{thermal}}$ at $t=200$. This is
qualitatively consistent with the early-time portion of a Page curve
\cite{Page1993}: entropy rises as Hawking radiation entangles with the
system. In this toy model the entropy does not decrease back to zero
because the finite-dimensional GKSL dynamics relaxes to a mixed thermal
fixed point, not a pure state---the Page curve turnover is expected only
in models where the black hole completely evaporates.

\begin{remark}[Fidelity Distinction]
We distinguish two fidelity quantities throughout. The Stinespring
fidelity $F$ (Table above) measures agreement between the GKSL channel
and its unitary dilation; this is $\geq 1-10^{-8}$. The thermalization
fidelity $F_{\mathrm{th}} = 0.9999192951$ (equation \eqref{eq:Fth})
measures how closely the steady state matches the ideal Gibbs state.
These are different quantities addressing different aspects of the model.
\end{remark}

\subsection{What TE2.4 Establishes}

To summarise precisely what the experiment proves:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \textit{Mathematical fact (known):} Every CPTP channel admits a
Stinespring dilation \cite{Stinespring1955}. The GKSL evaporation
channel $\Phi_{\Delta t}$ is CPTP (verified numerically), so a unitary
dilation $U$ exists.

\item \textit{Explicit construction (new):} An explicit Kraus-based
Stinespring construction is given for the JT-like PSC model with
$\dim\mathcal{H}_E = 7$, verified to fidelity $F\geq 1-10^{-8}$ on
three representative states.

\item \textit{Physical interpretation (new, within MFRR):} The operator
$\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ is identified as the physical realisation of the
recovery channel corresponding to this unitary dilation, within the PSC
framework.

\item \textit{Thermalization and entropy curve (new):} The JT-like PSC
model thermalises to within $0.008\%$ of the Gibbs state. The open
Markovian GKSL model, which models early-time Hawking evaporation into
an infinite bath, exhibits a monotonically rising entropy consistent with
the early-time Page behaviour; entropy does not turn over because the
radiation register is unbounded (see \S\,\ref{sec:scope}).

\item \textit{Operational $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ circuit---open-system
model (new):} The operator $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ is constructed explicitly
as the $56$-dimensional quantum circuit $U^\dagger$ (the Stinespring
adjoint). Applying $U^\dagger$ to the system+environment pair recovers
the initial vacuum state to fidelity $F = 0.9967$; the recovered state
has purity $0.993$ (SHA-256 \texttt{882cd67a\ldots},
\texttt{results/pt\_inverse\_circuit/}).

\item \textit{Full Page curve and exact $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ recovery---unitary
closed-system model, pure initial BH (new):} In the physically correct closed model---BH
as $n$~qubits in pure state $|{+}\rangle^{\otimes n}$, radiation as $n$~fresh
qubits in $|0\rangle^{\otimes n}$, evaporation via unitary partial-SWAP
operators $U_{\rm evap}$---we obtain the following for $n=4$ modes
(SHA-256 \texttt{7f132bb1\ldots}, \texttt{results/page\_curve\_unitary/}):
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item $S_{\rm BH}$ rises to $0.2458$~nats at the Page time, then falls to
    $0.000000$~nats: the \emph{full Page curve with turnover}.
  \item $S_{\rm BH} = S_{\rm rad}$ throughout, with total purity $= 1.000000$,
    confirming the evolution is exactly unitary.
  \item Applying $\mathrm{PT}^{-1} \equiv U_{\rm evap}^\dagger$ recovers the
    initial state to fidelity $F = 1.000000$ (exact by unitarity).
\end{itemize}
The finite radiation register is essential: once $\dim\mathcal{H}_{\rm rad\,filled}
> \dim\mathcal{H}_{\rm BH}$, complementarity of the pure total state forces
$S_{\rm BH}$ to decrease. This is the standard Page~\cite{Page1993} argument
realised explicitly within the MFRR framework.

\item \textit{Purified thermal BH---Page curve and exact $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$
recovery (new):} To make the closed model directly comparable to the initial
thermal state studied in the GKSL model, we introduce a reference system
$\mathcal{H}_R \cong \mathcal{H}_{\rm BH}$ that purifies the thermal BH
density matrix. The total initial state is $|\Phi^+\rangle_{{\rm BH},R}\otimes
|0\rangle^{\otimes n}_{\rm rad}$, where $|\Phi^+\rangle$ is the maximally
entangled state (i.e.\ the thermal BH is purified by its interior degrees of
freedom $R$); $R$ remains untouched during evaporation. This is precisely the
setup underlying the island formula. For $n=3$ modes (SHA-256
\texttt{e9ac80b0\ldots}, \texttt{results/page\_curve\_thermal/}; confirmed
independently for $n=4$, SHA-256 \texttt{3cbd71f2\ldots}):
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item $S_{\rm BH}$ starts at $\ln 8 = 2.079$~nats (thermal entropy), decreases
    monotonically as radiation absorbs BH entropy, and reaches $0$ after full
    evaporation: the \emph{Page curve for a thermal BH}.
  \item The Page crossing occurs when $S_{\rm rad} = S_{\rm BH}$ after $n/2$~modes
    have been emitted, in exact agreement with Page~\cite{Page1993}.
    For even $n$ this is an integer mode count (e.g.\ $n=4$: crossing at exactly
    2~modes); for odd $n$ (here $n=3$), $n/2 = 1.5$ is non-integer and the crossing
    occurs continuously between the $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$-th and $\lceil n/2\rceil$-th
    discrete mode emissions (confirmed numerically at $t = 30$ of $60$ simulation
    steps, i.e.\ $1.5$ modes).
  \item $S_R = \ln 8$ throughout; $S_{\rm rad}$ rises to $\ln 8$ at the end,
    encoding all the original BH thermal entropy. Total purity $= 1.000000$.
  \item $\mathrm{PT}^{-1} \equiv U_{\rm evap}^\dagger$ restores the BH--$R$
    maximally entangled state to fidelity $F = 1.000000$, recovering
    $S_{\rm BH} = \ln 8$: the thermal entropy is exactly returned.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

Items 1--2 are established within the toy model. Item~3 is a physical
interpretation within MFRR. Items~4--5 describe the Markovian (open) regime;
item~6 provides the full closed-system result for a pure BH; item~7 extends
it to the physically relevant thermal BH in the purified setup underlying the
island formula. None constitutes a proof that information is preserved in real
astrophysical black holes.

\begin{remark}[Open, closed-pure, and closed-thermal models]
The open GKSL model (items 1--5) is a Markovian approximation to early-time
Hawking evaporation; its infinite bath prevents Page-curve turnover. The
closed pure-state model (item~6) establishes the full Page curve for a BH
with initially zero entropy. The closed thermal model (item~7) is the
physically representative case: the BH starts fully thermalized, the initial
entropy is carried by entanglement with a reference $R$ (representing the BH
interior), and full evaporation transfers all entropy to radiation while
$\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ restores the original BH--$R$ entanglement exactly. All
three models are consistent with the Stinespring/MFRR framework and address
complementary physical regimes.
\end{remark}

\section{Relationship to Existing Approaches}
\label{sec:comparison}

\subsection{Island Formula and Quantum Extremal Surfaces}

The island formula \cite{Penington2020,AlmheiriEngelhartsMarolfMaxfield2019}
derives the Page curve in JT gravity coupled to a CFT bath by identifying
``island'' regions whose entropy is included in the fine-grained entropy
of Hawking radiation via the quantum extremal surface prescription. This
reproduces the Page curve turnover without requiring direct knowledge of
the microscopic recovery channel.

The MFRR approach differs in emphasis. The island calculation works in
the semiclassical path integral and requires a replica trick; the MFRR
approach works in the operator-algebraic language of CPTP maps and
makes the recovery channel explicit via $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$. The two
frameworks are complementary contributions to the information paradox:
the island formula delivers the thermodynamic entropy curve establishing
that information is not lost; the MFRR approach provides the explicit
operator-algebraic recovery mechanism at the Hilbert-space level.
A complete resolution of the information paradox would benefit from
both: the entropy curve (from island/QES calculations) and the recovery
operator (from MFRR/Stinespring). The present work contributes the
latter, for a toy model.

A notable limitation of our current model, by comparison with the island
formula, is that the JT-like PSC model is fully finite-dimensional and
does not contain the gravitational path integral structure that gives
rise to replica wormholes. Extension to that regime is an open problem.

\subsection{ER=EPR}

Maldacena and Susskind \cite{MaldacenaSusskind2013} proposed that
maximally entangled pairs of particles are connected by Einstein-Rosen
bridges, suggesting that entanglement and wormholes are dual descriptions
of the same phenomenon. The MFRR approach does not contradict ER=EPR;
both posit that information is geometrically or structurally encoded in
the environment degrees of freedom rather than destroyed. The $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$
mechanism can be seen as providing a dynamical account of the recovery
process whose existence ER=EPR motivates.

\subsection{Firewall Paradox}

The AMPS firewall argument \cite{AlmheiriMarolfPolchinskiSully2013} shows
that maintaining unitarity, the equivalence principle, and the no-drama
condition for infalling observers is inconsistent within standard
semiclassical assumptions. The MFRR framework does not resolve the
firewall paradox: we do not provide a model for the experience of an
infalling observer, nor do we address the equivalence principle at the
level of local field theory. The present work operates entirely at the
level of the system Hilbert space and its environment dilation; it does
not engage with the local vs.\ global description tension that underlies
the firewall.

\subsection{Page Curve}

The open GKSL model (items 1--4, \S\,\ref{sec:te24}) exhibits an
entropy curve rising from zero toward the thermal value, consistent
with early-time Hawking evaporation. The turnover is absent because
the infinite Markovian bath has a fixed-point attractor at
$\rho_{\mathrm{th}}$; it is not a counter-evidence to information
preservation but a feature of open-system dynamics.

The full Page curve --- both the rise and the fall to zero --- is
exhibited in two complementary closed unitary models. Item~6
(pure initial BH, $n=4$ qubits) demonstrates entropy turnover and
exact $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ recovery ($F=1.000000$). Item~7 (purified
thermal initial BH, $n=3$ and $n=4$ qubits, the island formula setup)
demonstrates the full thermal Page curve: $S_{\rm BH}$ starts at
$\log d_{\rm BH}$, transfers monotonically to radiation with the Page
crossing at $n/2$ emitted modes (exactly as predicted by
Page~\cite{Page1993}), and reaches zero at full evaporation.
$\mathrm{PT}^{-1} = U_{\rm evap}^\dagger$ then restores the BH--interior
entanglement exactly ($F=1.000000$).

The recovery is exact because the closed model is by construction
unitary: $U_{\rm evap}^\dagger U_{\rm evap} = I$. This is not a
proof that information is preserved in real astrophysical black holes,
but it demonstrates that within a finite-dimensional unitary framework
consistent with MFRR/PSC, the complete Page curve and exact information
recovery are achieved in the physically representative thermal BH setup.

\section{Scope, Limitations, and Open Fronts}
\label{sec:scope}

\subsection{Toy Model Limitations}

The JT-like PSC model is a finite-dimensional, Markovian, non-relativistic
toy model. It captures the qualitative structure of Hawking radiation
(thermal emission and absorption, detailed balance, CPTP dynamics) but
omits:
\begin{itemize}
\item The gravitational path integral and its non-perturbative saddles
  (replica wormholes, gravitational instantons).
\item The continuous spectrum of a genuine quantum field theory.
\item Back-reaction of radiation on the black hole geometry.
\item Relativistic and causal structure of the horizon.
\end{itemize}
Claims made in this paper are restricted to the toy model and its
mathematical properties.

\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$}{PT-inverse} Physical Realization}

The $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ operator is defined at the level of the MFRR
framework as a structural constraint on PSC-consistent histories. We have
not provided a Hilbert-space operator realisation of $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$
independent of the Stinespring construction. Establishing whether
$\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ can be identified with a specific quantum circuit or
gravitational operation in a more realistic model is an open problem.

\subsection{Extension to 3+1 Gravity}

The most important open front is the extension to $3{+}1$-dimensional
quantum gravity. In a realistic black hole, the system Hilbert space is
infinite-dimensional, the dynamics is not Markovian at all timescales,
and the notion of a preferred Hilbert space decomposition
$\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}} \otimes \mathcal{H}_E$ is itself ambiguous
(the factorisation problem). Addressing these issues requires tools
beyond those employed here.

\subsection{Non-Markovian Effects and Long-Time Unitarity}

The Stinespring dilation is constructed here for a single time step
$\Delta t = 0.01$. Over long timescales, GKSL dynamics with a fixed
Lindbladian is Markovian by assumption; genuine black hole evaporation
may exhibit non-Markovian memory effects that are not captured by the
GKSL model. The relationship between the step-wise Stinespring construction
and a global unitary for the full evaporation history is an open
technical question.

\subsection{Falsifiability}

The MFRR framework is falsifiable in the following senses. (i) If the
PSC condition were shown to be inconsistent with Standard Model physics
at low energies, the framework would be refuted (this has been examined
in other papers in the programme \cite{SpivackPhysicsPortal}). (ii) If
a no-go theorem were established showing that no Hilbert-space operator
can implement $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ in a relativistic setting consistent
with causality, the interpretive claim would be undermined. (iii) The
specific numerical predictions of the toy model (thermalization fidelity,
entropy curve, Stinespring dimension) are falsifiable within the model
and have been verified in TE2.4.

The abstract PSC record-consistency theorem provides machine-checked
formal backing for this framework: NEMS Paper~38
\cite{SpivackNEMS38} proves \texttt{BlackHoles.record\_consistency\_abstract}
in nems-lean (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19429792, zero \texttt{sorry}) ---
if two worlds disagree on an observation, the semantics is non-categorical
and a selector is required, ruling out genuine information destruction in a
PSC universe. The no-hypercomputing-from-black-holes theorem
(NEMS 35+38~\cite{SpivackNEMS35,SpivackNEMS38},
\texttt{BlackHoles.no\_hypercomputing\_from\_bh}, zero \texttt{sorry})
further establishes that no total-effective black-hole decoder can decide a
diagonal-capable predicate on the outcome space.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have examined the black hole information paradox through the lens of
the MFRR framework, proposing that \emph{reflexive unitarity}---the
structural requirement imposed by the PSC condition together with the
canonical operator $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$---provides a physical interpretation
of the unitary dilation guaranteed by the Stinespring theorem for any
CPTP Hawking evaporation channel.

The key mathematical fact is established: GKSL evaporation channels are
CPTP and therefore admit Stinespring dilations. The novel contributions
are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The MFRR interpretation of $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ as the explicit
  recovery operator identified with the Stinespring adjoint $U^\dagger$.
\item The explicit Stinespring construction for a JT-like PSC universe
  with $\dim\mathcal{H}_E = 7$, verified to fidelity $F\geq 1-10^{-8}$
  and thermalization fidelity $F_{\mathrm{th}} = 0.9999193$ (TE2.4).
\item An explicit PT$^{-1}$ quantum circuit recovering the vacuum
  from a partially-thermalized state to $F=0.9967$ in the open model.
\item The full Page curve with turnover and exact $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$
  recovery ($F=1.000000$) in a closed finite-qubit model (pure initial BH).
\item The complete Page curve for the \emph{thermal} initial BH in
  the island-formula setup (BH purified by reference $R$ representing
  the BH interior): entropy transfers monotonically from BH to radiation
  with the Page crossing at $n/2$ modes (exactly as Page~\cite{Page1993}
  predicted), and $\mathrm{PT}^{-1} = U_{\rm evap}^\dagger$ restores
  the BH--interior entanglement to $F=1.000000$.
\end{enumerate}

We have been explicit about what this work does not establish: it does
not prove that information is preserved in real astrophysical black holes,
does not resolve the firewall paradox, and does not provide a
first-principles derivation of $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$ beyond identification
with the Stinespring adjoint. These remain open problems requiring
extension to $3{+}1$ quantum gravity and non-Markovian dynamics.

The MFRR programme provides a structural framework in which the question
``which unitary implements information recovery?'' has a principled
and constructive answer: it is $U_{\rm evap}^\dagger$, the adjoint of
the evaporation unitary, identified physically as $\mathrm{PT}^{-1}$.
Within the toy model this answer is exact and reproducible.
Translating this principle into a concrete prediction for astrophysical
black holes is the primary open front of this programme.

\section{Stinespring Construction: Algorithmic Details}
\label{app:stinespring}

Given the GKSL master equation with Hamiltonian $H$ and Lindblad
operators $\{L_k\}_{k=1}^m$ at coupling strengths $\{\gamma_k\}$,
the Stinespring construction for time step $\Delta t$ proceeds as
follows:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Compute $K_0 = I - (iH + \frac{1}{2}\sum_k \gamma_k L_k^\dagger L_k)\Delta t$.
\item Compute $K_k = \sqrt{\gamma_k \Delta t}\,L_k$ for $k=1,\ldots,m$.
\item Verify completeness: $\sum_{k=0}^m K_k^\dagger K_k \approx I$
  (error of order $\Delta t^2$; for $\Delta t = 0.01$ this is $\sim
  10^{-4}$, consistent with first-order Kraus approximation).
\item Assemble the isometry $V$ as in equation~\eqref{eq:isometry}:
  a $(d_\mathcal{H}(m+1))\times d_\mathcal{H}$ matrix where the
  $k$-th block of $d_\mathcal{H}$ rows is $K_k$.
\item Extend $V$ to a unitary $U$ on the full space $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{sys}}
  \otimes \mathcal{H}_E$ (dimension $d_\mathcal{H}\times(m+1) = 56$)
  via QR decomposition and Gram-Schmidt completion of the orthogonal
  complement.
\item Verify: for each test state $\rho$, compute $\rho' = \mathrm{Tr}_E[U
  (\rho\otimes|0\rangle\langle 0|)U^\dagger]$ and compare with
  $\Phi_{\Delta t}(\rho) = \sum_k K_k \rho K_k^\dagger$ by computing
  the fidelity $F(\rho', \Phi_{\Delta t}(\rho))$.
\end{enumerate}

The first-order Kraus approximation (steps 1--2) is valid for small
$\Delta t$; for the exact propagator $\Phi_t = e^{t\mathcal{L}}$ the
Kraus operators are obtained from the matrix exponential of the
superoperator representation of $\mathcal{L}$.

\section{Lean Formalization of PSC Constraints on Black Hole Information}
\label{app:lean}

Two results in the nems-lean library (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19429710,
GitHub \url{https://github.com/novaspivack/nems-lean}) provide
machine-checked formal backing for the claims of this paper.
Both are proved with zero \texttt{sorry} and zero custom axioms in these proof chains; the
build command is \texttt{lake update \&\& lake build} from the nems-lean
root.

\paragraph{\texttt{BlackHoles.record\_consistency\_abstract} (NEMS Paper~38,
Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19429792).}
\textit{Statement:} Given a semantic structure where world $w$ satisfies
observation $o$ but world $w'$ does not (an ``information-erasing
appearance''), the observation semantics is non-categorical: at least
two world-types are distinguishable, and a selector is required.
Under PSC (which forbids external selectors), this rules out genuine
information destruction in a PSC universe.
\textit{Key proof step:} The proof formalises that a categorical
semantics would make $w$ and $w'$ observationally equivalent, contradicting
the erasing appearance. The conclusion that an internal selector must
exist then follows from the NEMS trichotomy.

\paragraph{\texttt{BlackHoles.no\_hypercomput\allowbreak ing\_from\_bh} (NEMS Papers~35+38,
Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19429786).}
\textit{Statement:} Under the SelectorStrength barrier premises
(extensional nontrivial predicate, anti-decider closure, fixed-point
hypothesis), no total-effective black-hole decoder can decide a
diagonal-capable predicate on the BH outcome space.
\textit{Key proof step:} Instantiates the general
\path{SelectorStrength.no_total_decider_at_strength_nontrivial}
barrier theorem (proved by diagonalisation) to the black-hole decoding
predicate class.
