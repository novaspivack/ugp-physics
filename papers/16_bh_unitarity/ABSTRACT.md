# Abstract

**Black Hole Unitarity via Reflexive Unitarity and Stinespring Dilation:
A GKSL Model in a JT-like PSC Universe**

Nova Spivack, 2026

---

The black hole information paradox asks whether gravitational collapse is
consistent with global quantum unitarity. We examine this question within
the Mathematical Foundations of Reflexive Reality (MFRR) framework, which
posits a canonical adjudication operator PT whose inverse PT⁻¹ implements
unitary information recovery at the level of the Persistent
Self-Consistency (PSC) condition. Within this framework, Hawking evaporation
is modelled as a GKSL master equation acting on a JT-like toy Hilbert space.
Since every completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) map admits a
Stinespring dilation — a mathematical theorem independent of any
gravitational model — the evaporation channel is unitarily equivalent to a
reversible evolution on an enlarged system-plus-environment space. The MFRR
contribution is interpretive and constructive: we provide a physical
mechanism (PT⁻¹) for understanding which unitary implements the recovery,
and we present an explicit construction of the Stinespring unitary for the
JT-like PSC model.

In the computational experiment TE2.4, the evaporation channel
Φ_{Δt} = e^{Δt·L} is constructed for a system of n=3 bosonic modes
(d=2 Fock levels each, total dimension d_H=8) at Hawking temperature
T_H ≈ 0.004. The environment Hilbert space satisfies dim(H_E) = 7 (one
plus the number of Lindblad operators). Numerical verification on vacuum,
Fock, and thermal test states yields Stinespring fidelity F ≥ 1 − 10⁻⁸;
the channel satisfies detailed balance to 0.00% error and the CPTP property
is verified via positive semidefiniteness of all evolved states. A Page-like
entropy curve is exhibited, with the steady-state density matrix achieving
fidelity 0.9999192951 with the ideal thermal state and the steady-state
entropy reaching 97.2% of the ideal thermal value.

We carefully distinguish what this toy model establishes from open problems:
extension to 3+1 quantum gravity, the firewall tension, and a Hilbert-space
realization of PT⁻¹ remain explicit open fronts.
