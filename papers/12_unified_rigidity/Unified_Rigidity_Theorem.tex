\begin{abstract}
We show that three independently developed rigidity mechanisms jointly select the same canonical
physical branch under an explicit and audited premise bundle.
\textbf{Layer I/II PSC theory-space selection}~\cite{SpivackPhysicsPortal,Spivack2025_FirstPrinciplesSM}
narrows the space of 4D renormalizable gauge theories to the Standard Model gauge structure and
three generations; this is supported by a \emph{finite exhaustive enumeration} over
$34{,}560$ candidate universes across 12 gauge groups including Pati-Salam and $E_6$
(TE2.2 extended scan: SM ranks~\#1, only $0.03\%$ pass the hard PSC filters,
all survivors are SM-like, all BSM candidates decisively eliminated~\cite{SpivackTE22Scan}).
\textbf{UGP arithmetic and seed rigidity}~\cite{Spivack2025_UGPUniqueness,spivack2025main_ugp_paper}
is fully machine-checked in \texttt{ugp-lean}~\cite{ugp-lean}: the RSUC theorem isolates
the Lepton Seed $(1,73,823)$ as the unique MDL-minimal survivor of the two-stage sieve
(0~sorrys, 0~custom axioms).
\textbf{Dynamical basin structure} places the Lepton Seed and its mirror in the canonical
basin~\textbf{A} across all tested configurations, while off-residual seeds fall in
basins~\textbf{B}/\textbf{C}; this is frozen and SHA-256 certified.
A new Lean bridge library, \texttt{unified-rigidity-lean}~\cite{unified-rigidity-lean},
formalizes the common admissibility framework and proves \texttt{unified\_rigidity\_theorem}
under the explicit premise bundle recorded in its assumption ledger.
Every claim is strictly typed: theorem-extracted, bridge, computational/certified, or
residual/open.
The result is a conditional synthesis theorem under an explicit premise bundle; every
assumption is typed and audited.
The formal PSC$\to$SM step is now unconditional: the Residual Classification (RCC) is established as a theorem (\texttt{PSC.RCCInfiniteFamilies}, zero \texttt{sorry}~\cite{ugp-lean});
the all-$n$ UGP extension is now proved (\texttt{asymptotic\_sparsity\_universal}, zero sorry~\cite{Spivack2026_Uniqueness25}); the neutrino mass-squared splitting ratio $\Delta m^2_{21}/\Delta m^2_{31}$ is predicted to $0.52\%$ from Braid Atlas $b$-values via the formula $m_{\nu_g} \propto b_g^{N_c + \theta_{\rm Koide}} = b_g^{29/9}$, with normal ordering automatic and no external anchor (companion paper~\cite{Spivack2026_SM_UGP}).
Any construction of a PSC-admissible theory outside the SM signature, a non-Lepton-Seed UGP
survivor satisfying the full sieve, or a canonical-seed trajectory failing the predicted basin
would falsify the result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The program has produced strong but distributed results: gauge-theoretic closure and PSC
narratives in MFRR and the Physics Portal~\cite{SpivackPhysicsPortal,SpivackNEMSMFRRBridge},
arithmetic and seed classification in the UGP uniqueness and monograph

and dynamical attractor structure in the dynamics/universality
manuscript~\cite{spivack2025dynamics_universality}.
This paper does not add a fourth domain derivation; it states the \emph{common canonical
branch} selected by the intersection of these mechanisms, establishes the formal bridge
connecting them, and gives an audited account of what is proved, what is certified, and
what remains open.
The three mechanisms are logically independent in construction: PSC operates in theory space,
UGP operates in arithmetic seed space, and dynamics operates in trajectory space.
Their convergence on the same canonical branch therefore constitutes nontrivial cross-domain
agreement, not a circular derivation.
The `independence' is methodological---PSC sieve, arithmetic sieve, and RG basin analysis
use different mathematical tools---but not ontological: the dynamics engine applies GTE
update rules derived from the same UGP arithmetic that defines the seed.
The concordance demonstrates internal coherence of the UGP Physics programme under three
evaluation criteria, not convergence from three entirely unrelated starting points.

\section{Residuals and controlled tensions}

\paragraph{RCC and scope of PSC closure.}
The PSC layer is formalized in Lean at the level of explicit predicates encoding the Paper~05
sieve conditions.

is now unconditional: the RCC is established as a Lean-certified theorem

and now constitutes part of a multi-pronged proof over all topologies.

\paragraph{All-$n$ uniqueness.}
The UGP sieve is certified at $n=10$ and related finite levels in Lean with 0~sorrys.
The present work relies only on these formally proved finite-level results and does not assume
global uniqueness.
The all-$n$ extension is proved: \texttt{asymptotic\_sparsity\_universal}~\cite{Spivack2026_Uniqueness25,ugp-lean} (zero sorry, all $n\in\mathbb{N}$).

\paragraph{Neutrino sector.}
Masses and mixing angles are not closed theorem-level consequences of the seed; they remain a
predictive fork testable against experiment~\cite{ParticleDataGroup2022}.

\paragraph{Dynamics assumptions.}
The dynamical layer is not presented as a theorem-level derivation.
Basin claims are packaged in \texttt{DynamicsAssumptions}: four explicitly named fields,
each audited against frozen discovery-lab exports (SHA-256 provenance in the assumption
ledger)~\cite{unified-rigidity-lean,ugp-physics-repo}.
This is an explicit and checkable computational commitment, not a formal Lean proof.

\paragraph{Physical completeness.}
The present result is a rigidity statement about the structure of admissible theories under
the stated premise bundle; it is not a complete derivation of all physical sectors.
The framework is open to extension as further layers are formalized.

\paragraph{Inherited residuals from companion papers.}
Three open fronts in companion papers affect the overall programme but are decoupled from
the core synthesis here:

  \item \emph{Elegant Kernel constants} ($k_L^2$, $k_{\mathrm{gen}}^2$): \textbf{closed}---all nine UCL Elegant Kernel coefficients are Lean-certified unconditional structural consequences of the GTE substrate (theorems \texttt{thm\_ucl1/2/4/5\_fully\_unconditional}, \texttt{thm\_ucl\_3}, \texttt{k\_L2\_eq}, \texttt{quarterLockLaw}; zero \texttt{sorry}, zero UGP-specific axioms).     derivations is a postulate at a different ridge level, not a direct logical consequence

  \item \emph{$\delta_{\mathrm{UGP}}$ Stage~2 non-circularity}: whether the UGP delta formula
    is derivable from the seed without circular use of the final value is a pending

\end{enumerate}
None of these affect the unified rigidity conclusion, which depends only on the formally
proved components (IDs 2.1--3.3 in Table~\ref{tab:assumption-ledger}) and the explicit
dynamics axiom (ID~4.0).
