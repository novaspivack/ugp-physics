What am I looking at?

A real scalar field Φ with seven degenerate vacua Φk = 2πk/7. A kink is a moving boundary between two vacuum colors — width 1/m ≈ 0.111 fm, rest mass (8/49)m = 290.10 MeV. Net winding (a Z7 charge) maps composite sectors to particle quantum numbers.

EXACT Pure sector: integrable dynamics — collisions are always transmissive; no annihilation, no radiation. Transient dips in the kink census during overlap are level-crossing occlusion, not annihilation.

PROXY Perturbed mode: an integrability-breaking perturbation (double-sine-Gordon proxy, PROVISIONAL). Slow kink–antikink pairs (vrel ≲ 0.10 at η = 0.05) capture into oscillating bions and decay by radiation — genuine annihilation.

PROVEN 3D view: no compact, finite-energy 3D particle exists in this field theory — two general theorems rule out every tested shape. GTE particles are Fock-space excitations certified by an extended classical background instead (Lean-certified, zero sorry). The view renders that certified extended structure — a single domain wall, or three walls meeting along an axis — as a static illustration only, never a claim of a localized particle.

2D scalar sector: topological objects are domain walls, not point particles.
