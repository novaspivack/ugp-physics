# Project Status

**Last updated: November 2026**

## Summary

This repository is part of the UGP Physics research programme — an attempt to derive aspects of fundamental physics from a small set of first principles, with select results formalized and machine-checked in Lean 4.

**This programme is not complete, and its central claims are not currently validated.** A systematic internal audit — prompted by continued scrutiny of the work and by reader feedback — has identified significant, unresolved problems in both the physics papers and the Lean formalizations. Some of these are substantial enough that key results in their current form cannot be relied upon.

## What this means in practice

- **Every paper, proof, and claim here is provisional.** None should be cited or relied upon as settled until this notice is updated or removed.
- **Some results will be revised. Some may be qualified or withdrawn.** The audit is ongoing; its outcome for any specific claim isn't yet determined.
- **A Lean proof's formal correctness does not, by itself, establish that the physics it accompanies is correct** — it only establishes that the stated premises imply the stated conclusion. The audit has found cases where the premises or physical interpretation were the problem, not the formal logic.
- **Updated papers will be published openly once specific issues are resolved**, or specific claims will be formally retracted if they can't be.

## Why this notice exists

Ambitious theoretical programmes frequently require major revision, and some don't survive close scrutiny — that's a normal, expected part of how theoretical work gets tested, not a unique failure. This was a genuine, serious attempt to construct a principled derivation of physical structure from first principles. The audit now underway is being taken seriously, including everywhere it finds that a specific result doesn't hold up. This notice stays current as that work proceeds.
